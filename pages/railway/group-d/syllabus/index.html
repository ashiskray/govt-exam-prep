RRB Group D CBT Exam Pattern

The RRB Group D Level-1 CBT consists of 100 objective-type questions. The section-wise distribution is indicative and may vary in the actual question paper.

Subject Questions Marks
General Science 25 25
Mathematics 25 25
General Intelligence & Reasoning 30 30
General Awareness & Current Affairs 20 20
Total 100 100

CBT Highlights

100 Questions

Each question carries 1 mark.

90 Minutes

The standard CBT duration is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

Objective Type

The questions are multiple-choice objective type questions.

Selection Process

The recruitment process under CEN 09/2025 comprises CBT, PET, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage 1 — CBT

Computer Based Test based on Mathematics, Reasoning, General Science and General Awareness & Current Affairs.

Stage 2 — PET

Candidates shortlisted from CBT undergo the qualifying Physical Efficiency Test.

Stage 3 — DV

Original certificates and documents are verified.

Stage 4 — Medical

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standard for the opted post.

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

PET is qualifying in nature and is conducted after CBT for candidates shortlisted according to the recruitment rules.

Candidate Weight Carrying Running
Male Carry 35 Kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down. Run 1000 metres in 4 minutes 15 seconds.
Female Carry 20 Kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down. Run 1000 metres in 5 minutes 40 seconds.

Important Syllabus Notes

  • The syllabus topics listed in the official CEN are illustrative and not necessarily exhaustive.
  • General Science covers Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences of 10th standard level (CBSE).
  • General Awareness includes current affairs in Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics, Politics and other subjects of importance.
  • The section-wise CBT distribution is indicative and there may be variation in the actual question paper.
  • There is negative marking of 1/3 mark for each wrong answer.

Medium of Examination

English & Hindi

CBT questions are available in English and Hindi.

Regional Languages

Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu are also available.

Language Selection

The candidate must select the examination medium from the languages provided in the online application.

How to Prepare

Mathematics

Build strong fundamentals in arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry and the remaining notified topics.

Reasoning

Practise series, coding-decoding, analogy, classification, directions, syllogism and analytical reasoning regularly.

Science

Revise Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at the 10th standard CBSE level.

Current Affairs

Follow current affairs related to Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics and Politics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many subjects are there in RRB Group D CBT?

There are four sections: General Science, Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and General Awareness & Current Affairs.

How many questions are asked in the CBT?

The indicative CBT pattern contains 100 questions carrying 100 marks.

What is the duration of the RRB Group D CBT?

The standard examination duration is 90 minutes. It is 120 minutes for candidates eligible for use of a scribe.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the CBT.

What is the General Science syllabus?

The General Science syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at the 10th standard CBSE level.

What is the Mathematics syllabus?

It includes Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock, Pipes & Cistern and other notified topics.

What is the Reasoning syllabus?

It includes Analogies, Alphabetical and Number Series, Coding and Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation and Sufficiency, Conclusions, Decision Making, Similarities and Differences, Analytical Reasoning, Classification, Directions, Statement-Arguments and Assumptions.

What is the General Awareness syllabus?

It covers current affairs in Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics, Politics and any other subject of importance.
