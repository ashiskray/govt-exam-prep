Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

10th Pass or ITI or equivalent or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT.

Nationality

Indian citizen or other eligible categories specified in the official notification.

Age

18–33 Years
(As on 01-01-2026)

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the medical standards prescribed for the particular Level-1 post.

Educational Qualification

Level-1 Posts

Candidates must possess the prescribed qualification on or before the closing date of application. The post parameters in Annexure-A specify 10th pass or ITI or equivalent or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT.

Important Note

Candidates waiting for the final result of the prescribed educational qualification are not eligible to apply. The qualification must have been obtained by the closing date of application.

Course Completed Act Apprentices

Course Completed Act Apprentices trained in Railway Establishments have specific provisions in the notification, including exemption from the Physical Efficiency Test as prescribed.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Tibetan refugee who came to India before 01.01.1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Person of Indian origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India, subject to the conditions prescribed in the notification.
  • Where a Certificate of Eligibility is required, it must be issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 33 Years

The age of candidates should be between 18 and 33 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to the post parameters and age-relaxation rules given in CEN No. 09/2025.

Age relaxation is available to eligible reserved categories and other specified categories as per the rules mentioned in the official notification.

Examination Pattern

RRB Group D Level-1 recruitment consists of a Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification and Medical Examination. The notification also reserves the right to conduct the CBT in single-stage or multi-stage mode.

Stage - 1

Computer Based Test (CBT)

The CBT is an objective type multiple-choice examination. The notification specifies 100 questions of one mark each.

Important Highlights

Online Exam

CBT is conducted in computer-based mode.

100 Questions

There are 100 objective questions in the CBT.

90 Minutes

Standard examination duration is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark is deducted.
  • No marks are awarded or deducted for unanswered questions.
  • Questions are objective type Multiple Choice Questions.
  • The section-wise distribution mentioned in the notification is indicative and may vary in the actual question paper.
  • Minimum qualifying percentages are specified separately for different communities in the notification.
Stage - 2

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

Candidates qualifying in the CBT are shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test. PET is followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Candidate Weight Carrying Running
Male Candidates Lift and carry 35 Kg weight for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down. Run 1000 metres in 4 minutes 15 seconds.
Female Candidates Lift and carry 20 Kg weight for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down. Run 1000 metres in 5 minutes 40 seconds.

PET Important Points

Point Details
PET Stages Weight carrying test followed by running test after a recovery gap.
Weight Test Candidate must carry the specified weight over 100 metres within the prescribed time without putting it down.
Course Completed Act Apprentices Exempted from Physical Efficiency Test as prescribed in the notification.
Ex-Servicemen Exempted from PET as prescribed in the notification.
PwBD Candidates PwBD candidates specified in the notification are exempted from appearing in PET, subject to the applicable conditions.

Recruitment Process

CBT

Computer Based Test is the written examination stage.

PET

Candidates shortlisted through CBT undergo the Physical Efficiency Test.

Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates are required to undergo Document Verification.

Medical Examination

Candidates must pass the prescribed Railway medical standards for the opted post.

Post Specific Medical Requirements

Medical standards differ according to the Level-1 post notified in CEN No. 09/2025.

Medical Standard General Requirement
A-2 Physically fit in all respects with prescribed distant and near vision standards and applicable colour, binocular, night and mesopic vision tests.
A-3 Physically fit in all respects with prescribed vision standards and applicable colour, binocular, night and mesopic vision tests.
B-1 Physically fit in all respects with prescribed distant and near vision requirements and applicable vision tests.
C-1 Physically fit in all respects with prescribed distant and near vision requirements.

Medical Examination

  • Candidates called for Document Verification have to pass the requisite medical fitness test conducted by the Railway Administration.
  • Visual acuity is an important criterion of Railway medical fitness.
  • The medical standard depends upon the particular post.
  • Candidates should ensure that they satisfy the medical requirements of the post they have opted for.
  • Candidates found medically unsuitable for the opted post will not be given an alternative appointment as stated in the notification.
  • The official notification advises candidates to refer to the applicable Railway medical standards before applying.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Read the official CEN No. 09/2025 carefully before applying.
  • Carry the required original photo identity proof to the examination centre.
  • Follow the examination instructions printed on the e-call letter and official RRB website.
  • Do not carry prohibited electronic devices or unfair means material to the examination venue.
  • Calculators and other electronic gadgets are not permitted inside the examination premises.
  • Keep your registered mobile number and email active throughout the recruitment process.
  • Follow the prescribed instructions for CBT, PET, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB Group D?

RRB Group D refers to recruitment for various Level-1 posts in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix of Indian Railways.

What is the educational qualification?

The notified Level-1 posts require 10th pass or ITI or equivalent or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT, as prescribed in Annexure-A.

What is the age limit?

The prescribed age is 18–33 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to applicable age-relaxation rules.

How many vacancies are notified?

CEN No. 09/2025 notifies a total of 22,195 vacancies for various Level-1 posts.

What is the initial basic pay?

The initial pay mentioned in the notification is ₹18,000 under Level-1 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer in the CBT.

Is PET compulsory?

Candidates qualifying in the CBT are required to undergo PET, subject to exemptions and provisions specified in the official notification.

What is the PET standard for male candidates?

Male candidates have to lift and carry 35 Kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down and run 1000 metres in 4 minutes 15 seconds.

What is the PET standard for female candidates?

Female candidates have to lift and carry 20 Kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes without putting the weight down and run 1000 metres in 5 minutes 40 seconds.

What are the stages of selection?

The recruitment process comprises Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination (ME).
