Post & Force

Railway Protection Force (RPF)

  • Constable (Executive) post under the Railway Protection Force.
  • The recruitment is conducted through the Railway Recruitment Boards.
  • Selected candidates are required to undergo the prescribed recruitment and training requirements.
  • Posting is subject to the requirements of the competent railway authority.

Railway Protection Special Force (RPSF)

  • The notified Constable (Executive) recruitment also covers the Railway Protection Special Force.
  • Candidates are selected through the prescribed RRB recruitment process.
  • Final appointment is subject to fulfilment of eligibility, medical and other prescribed conditions.
  • Training and service requirements apply as prescribed by the competent authority.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

10th Pass or Equivalent from a recognised Board.

Nationality

The candidate must be a citizen of India.

Age

18–25 Years as the standard prescribed age, with applicable relaxation provisions.

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed B-1 medical standard.

Educational Qualification

Constable (Executive)

Candidates must have passed 10th standard or an equivalent examination from a recognised Board.

Important Note

Candidates waiting for the final result of the prescribed educational qualification are not eligible to apply. The prescribed qualification must be possessed within the eligibility requirements of the recruitment.

Nationality

  • A candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • Proof of nationality and other eligibility details may be verified from original documents during Document Verification.
  • Eligibility is provisional until the required documents are verified.

Age Limit

18 – 25 Years

The standard prescribed age for Constable (Executive) is 18 to 25 years, subject to the applicable age-relaxation provisions for eligible categories.

The recruitment notice also contained a one-time upper-age relaxation provision. Candidates should always check the applicable recruitment notice for the age rules of the recruitment in which they apply.

Examination Pattern

The RPF Constable recruitment process consists of a Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Measurement Test (PMT) and Document Verification (DV).

Stage - 1

Computer Based Test (CBT)

The CBT is an objective-type multiple-choice examination at 10th / Matriculation level.

Total Marks 120
Duration 90 Minutes

Important Highlights

Online Exam

CBT is conducted in computer-based mode.

120 Questions

The CBT contains 120 objective-type questions.

90 Minutes

The standard CBT duration is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark is deducted.
  • No marks are awarded or deducted for questions that are not attempted.
  • The CBT consists of objective-type multiple-choice questions.
  • Normalization of marks is carried out when CBTs are held in multiple shifts.
  • Minimum qualifying percentage is 35% for UR, EWS and OBC-NCL candidates and 30% for SC and ST candidates.
Stage - 2

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

Candidates shortlisted on the basis of CBT performance are called for PET/PMT and Document Verification. PET is qualifying in nature and carries no marks.

Category Running Long Jump High Jump
Male 1600 metres in 5 minutes 45 seconds 14 feet 4 feet
Female 800 metres in 3 minutes 40 seconds 9 feet 3 feet

PET Important Points

Point Details
PET Nature Qualifying in nature; no marks are awarded.
Running Male candidates: 1600 metres in 5 minutes 45 seconds. Female candidates: 800 metres in 3 minutes 40 seconds.
Long Jump Male: 14 feet; Female: 9 feet.
High Jump Male: 4 feet; Female: 3 feet.
Number of Chances One chance is provided for the running event; up to two chances are provided for long jump and high jump.
Ex-Servicemen Ex-servicemen are exempted from PET but have to undergo PMT as prescribed.

Medical Examination

  • The prescribed medical standard for Constable (Executive) is B-1.
  • Candidates called for Document Verification have to pass the requisite medical fitness test conducted by the Railway Administration.
  • Visual acuity is an important criterion of Railway medical fitness.
  • Candidates with glasses, flat foot, knock knee, squint eyes, colour blindness and other specified bodily infirmities are not eligible for appointment as stated in the notification.
  • Being sent for medical examination does not by itself guarantee appointment or inclusion in the final select list.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Read the complete RPF Constable recruitment notification carefully before applying.
  • Apply only through the official RRB online application system.
  • Keep your registered mobile number and personal email ID active throughout the recruitment process.
  • Carry the prescribed e-call letter and valid original photo identity proof to the CBT.
  • Do not carry prohibited electronic devices or other banned items to the examination centre.
  • Follow the instructions issued by the concerned RRB for CBT, PET/PMT and Document Verification.
  • Multiple applications can lead to rejection and debarment as prescribed in the notification.

Fee Payment

  • Fee payment is made through online mode only.
  • Accepted online modes include internet banking, debit/credit cards and UPI.
  • Applicable service charges are borne by the candidate.
  • Only candidates who appear in the CBT are eligible for the applicable refund described in the notification.
  • EBC and EWS are distinct categories; EBC is relevant to fee concession and should not be confused with EWS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RPF Constable?

RPF Constable (Executive) is the constable-level recruitment post in the Railway Protection Force and Railway Protection Special Force.

What is the educational qualification?

The prescribed minimum qualification is 10th Pass or Equivalent from a recognised Board.

What is the standard age limit?

The standard prescribed age is 18–25 years, subject to applicable age-relaxation provisions.

How many questions are in the CBT?

The CBT has 120 objective-type questions to be attempted in 90 minutes.

What subjects are asked in the CBT?

Arithmetic, General Intelligence & Reasoning and General Awareness.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. One-third of a mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

What are the stages of selection?

The recruitment process comprises CBT, PET/PMT and Document Verification.

What is the PET standard for male candidates?

Male candidates must complete 1600 metres in 5 minutes 45 seconds, a 14-foot long jump and a 4-foot high jump.

What is the PET standard for female candidates?

Female candidates must complete 800 metres in 3 minutes 40 seconds, a 9-foot long jump and a 3-foot high jump.

What is the medical standard?

The prescribed medical standard for Constable (Executive) is B-1.

What is the initial basic pay?

The initial basic pay is ₹21,700 under Pay Level-3.
