RPF Constable CBT Exam Pattern

The RPF Constable CBT is an objective-type examination at 10th/Matric level. The official notification specifies 120 questions for 120 marks with a duration of 90 minutes.

CBT Highlights

120 Questions

Each correct answer carries 1 mark.

90 Minutes

The total CBT duration is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

Minimum Pass Percentage

35% for UR, EWS & OBC-NCL and 30% for SC & ST.

Selection Process

The RPF Constable recruitment process comprises Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET)/Physical Measurement Test (PMT), and Document Verification (DV).

Stage 1 — CBT

Objective-type Computer Based Test at 10th/Matric level. CBT marks are counted for shortlisting.

Stage 2 — PET/PMT

Candidates shortlisted on CBT merit undergo the qualifying Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Measurement Test.

Stage 3 — DV

Original certificates and documents are verified after qualifying the preceding stages.

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

PET is mandatory and qualifying in nature. No marks are awarded for PET.

Important Syllabus Notes

  • The CBT standard is 10th/Matric level.
  • Arithmetic carries 35 marks, General Intelligence & Reasoning carries 35 marks, and General Awareness carries 50 marks.
  • Questions are objective type with multiple choice answers.
  • 1/3 mark is deducted for every wrong answer; no marks are awarded or deducted for an unattempted question.
  • Normalization of marks will be done for CBTs held in multiple shifts.
  • Minimum pass percentage for eligibility is 35% for UR, EWS and OBC-NCL and 30% for SC & ST.
  • The syllabus topics are stated in the official notification as topics the questions are likely to cover; the list uses the notification's wording.

Medium of Examination

English & Hindi

Syllabus Coverage

Arithmetic

Cover every arithmetic topic listed in the official notification, especially number operations, percentages, ratio & proportion, averages, interest, profit & loss, mensuration and time & distance.

Reasoning

Practise the notified reasoning areas including analogies, spatial concepts, problem solving, classification, series, coding-decoding, statement-conclusion and syllogistic reasoning.

General Awareness

Prepare current events and the notified areas of Indian history, art & culture, geography, economics, polity, Constitution, sports and general science.

Exam Discipline

Keep the 90-minute duration, 1/3 negative marking and minimum qualifying percentages in mind while practising CBT questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many subjects are there in RPF Constable CBT?

There are three sections: Arithmetic, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and General Awareness.

How many questions are asked in the CBT?

The official notification specifies 120 objective-type questions carrying 120 marks.

What is the duration of the RPF Constable CBT?

The CBT duration is 90 minutes.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for every wrong answer. No marks are awarded or deducted for an unattempted question.

What is the Arithmetic syllabus?

It covers Number System, Whole Numbers, Decimals & Fractions and relationship between numbers, fundamental arithmetical operations, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Interest, Profit & Loss, Discount, Use of Tables & Graphs, Mensuration, Time & Distance and other related topics.

What is the General Intelligence & Reasoning syllabus?

It covers Analogies, Similarities & Differences, Spatial Visualization, Spatial Orientation, Problem Solving, Analysis, Judgement, Decision Making, Visual Memory, Discriminating Observation, Relationship concepts, Arithmetical Reasoning, Verbal & Figure classification, Arithmetic number series, Non-verbal series, Coding & Decoding, Statement-Conclusion and Syllogistic reasoning.

What is the General Awareness syllabus?

It covers the environment around the candidate and its application to society, current events, everyday observation and experience, Indian History, Art & Culture, Geography, Economics, General Polity, Indian Constitution, Sports, General Science and other subjects of importance.

Is PET qualifying or does it carry marks?

Passing PET is mandatory and PET is qualifying in nature. No marks are awarded for PET.

What are the RPF Constable PET standards?

Male candidates: 1600 metres in 5 minutes 45 seconds, long jump 14 ft and high jump 4 ft. Female candidates: 800 metres in 3 minutes 40 seconds, long jump 9 ft and high jump 3 ft.
