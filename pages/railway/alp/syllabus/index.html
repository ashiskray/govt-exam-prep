RRB ALP CBT Exam Pattern

The official CEN No. 01/2026 recruitment process includes CBT-1, CBT-2, CBAT, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage Part / Subject Questions Duration
CBT-1 Mathematics + Mental Ability + General Science 75 60 Minutes
CBT-2 Part-A Mathematics + General Intelligence & Reasoning + Basic Science & Engineering 100 90 Minutes
CBT-2 Part-B Relevant Trade / Subject 75 60 Minutes
CBT-2 Total 175 150 Minutes

CBT Highlights

CBT-1 — 75 Questions

75 questions carrying 75 marks. CBT-1 is a screening examination for shortlisting to CBT-2.

CBT-2 — 175 Questions

Part-A has 100 questions in 90 minutes and Part-B has 75 questions in 60 minutes.

1/3 Negative Marking

One-third mark is deducted for every wrong answer in CBT-1 and CBT-2.

Part-B is Qualifying

Part-B requires 35% qualifying marks for all candidates, irrespective of category.

Selection Process

The RRB ALP recruitment process under CEN No. 01/2026 comprises CBT-1, CBT-2, CBAT, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage 1 — CBT-1

Screening CBT covering Mathematics, Mental Ability and General Science. CBT-1 marks are not counted in the final panel.

Stage 2 — CBT-2

Two-part examination: Part-A tests common subjects and Part-B tests the relevant technical trade or selected subject.

Stage 3 — CBAT

Computer Based Aptitude Test for shortlisted candidates who qualify Part-B. Each test battery must be cleared separately.

Stage 4 — DV

Original certificates and documents are verified for candidates shortlisted on merit.

Stage 5 — Medical

Candidates must pass the prescribed A-1 medical standard for ALP.

CBT-2 Part-B Trade / Subject Selection

The notification states that Part-B is based on DGT-prescribed trade syllabi. The following grouping is given directly in the official CEN.

Engineering Qualification Trade / Subject of Choice for Part-B
Electrical Engineering and combination of various streams of Electrical Engineering Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Wireman, Armature & Coil Winder, Refrigeration and Air-conditioning Mechanic
Electronics Engineering and combination of various streams of Electronics Engineering Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic (Radio & TV)
Mechanical Engineering and combination of various streams of Mechanical Engineering Fitter, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel), Turner, Machinist, Refrigeration and Air-conditioning Mechanic, Heat Engine, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic
Automobile Engineering and combination of various streams of Automobile Engineering Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel), Heat Engine, Refrigeration and Air-conditioning

CBAT Syllabus & Requirements

  • Candidates equal to 8 times the number of ALP vacancies for each notified community/category are shortlisted for CBAT, subject to qualifying Part-B.
  • It is mandatory to clear each CBAT test battery/section separately.
  • CBAT is conducted only in English and Hindi.
  • There is no negative marking in CBAT.
  • All candidates, irrespective of community, must secure a minimum T-score of 42 marks in each test battery separately to qualify.
  • The official notification directs candidates to RDSO for information relating to the CBAT.
  • Shortlisted candidates must produce the prescribed Self Screening Medical Declaration Report in original on the CBAT day.

Important Syllabus Notes

  • CBT-1 is only a screening examination and its marks are not counted while preparing the final panel.
  • CBT-1 has 75 questions and 75 marks with a 60-minute duration.
  • CBT-2 has 175 questions in total: Part-A has 100 questions in 90 minutes and Part-B has 75 questions in 60 minutes.
  • There is 1/3 negative marking for each wrong answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2.
  • In CBT-2, only Part-A marks are counted for further shortlisting, provided the candidate qualifies Part-B with 35% marks.
  • Part-B is a qualifying test and uses the relevant DGT-prescribed trade syllabus.
  • The official CEN does not reproduce the complete DGT trade-wise detailed syllabus; it directs candidates to the Directorate General of Training (DGT) website for the syllabi of different technical trades.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RRB ALP CBT-1 syllabus?

CBT-1 covers Mathematics, Mental Ability and General Science. Mathematics includes Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock and Pipes & Cistern. Mental Ability includes analogies, series, coding-decoding, mathematical operations, relationships, syllogism, jumbling, Venn Diagram, data interpretation and sufficiency, conclusions and decision making, similarities and differences, analytical reasoning, classification, directions, statement-arguments and assumptions. General Science covers Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences of 10th standard level.

What is the RRB ALP CBT-2 Part-A syllabus?

Part-A contains Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning and Basic Science & Engineering. Basic Science & Engineering includes Engineering Drawing, Units, Measurements, Mass Weight and Density, Work Power and Energy, Speed and Velocity, Heat and Temperature, Basic Electricity, Levers and Simple Machines, Occupational Safety and Health, Environment Education and IT Literacy.

What is the CBT-2 Part-B syllabus?

Part-B is a qualifying test based on the relevant trade syllabus prescribed by the Directorate General of Training (DGT). ITI/Trade Apprenticeship candidates appear in the section for their trade. Degree and Diploma candidates select one trade from the list associated with their engineering discipline.

How many questions are in CBT-2?

CBT-2 contains 175 questions in total. Part-A has 100 questions and Part-B has 75 questions. The total duration is 2 hours 30 minutes: 90 minutes for Part-A and 60 minutes for Part-B.

Is Part-B of CBT-2 qualifying?

Yes. Part-B is qualifying in nature. Candidates must secure 35% marks in Part-B irrespective of category/community. Only Part-A marks are counted for further shortlisting, subject to qualifying Part-B.

Is there negative marking in RRB ALP?

Yes. There is negative marking of 1/3 mark for every wrong answer in CBT-1 and CBT-2. There is no negative marking in CBAT.

What is the CBAT requirement for ALP?

Candidates shortlisted for CBAT must clear each test battery separately. All candidates must secure a minimum T-score of 42 marks in each test battery separately to qualify. CBAT is conducted only in English and Hindi and has no negative marking.

Does CBT-1 marks count in the final merit?

No. CBT-1 is only a screening examination for shortlisting candidates to CBT-2. The marks of CBT-1 are not counted while preparing the final panel.

Which trades are listed for Electrical Engineering candidates?

The official CEN lists Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Wireman, Armature & Coil Winder, and Refrigeration and Air-conditioning Mechanic for Electrical Engineering and its combinations.

Which trades are listed for Mechanical Engineering candidates?

The official CEN lists Fitter, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel), Turner, Machinist, Refrigeration and Air-conditioning Mechanic, Heat Engine and Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic.
