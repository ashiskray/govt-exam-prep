Post Details

RRB CEN No. 01/2026 notified the following post.

Assistant Loco Pilot

ALP is the single post notified under CEN No. 01/2026, with Level-2 pay, ₹19,900 initial pay and A-1 medical standard.

Technical Eligibility

Eligibility can be through specified ITI trades, Course Completed Act Apprenticeship, or the prescribed engineering Diploma / Degree route.

A-1 Medical Standard

The post has stringent medical and vision requirements prescribed in the notification.

CBAT Requirement

Eligible candidates must qualify the Computer Based Aptitude Test before final merit and Document Verification.

Salary

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Matriculation / SSLC with the prescribed ITI, apprenticeship, Diploma or Degree qualification.

Nationality

Eligible candidates must satisfy the nationality / citizenship conditions prescribed in the official CEN.

Age

18–30 Years
(As on 01-07-2026)

Medical Fitness

A-1 medical standard with prescribed vision and other medical requirements.

Educational Qualification

Route A — Matriculation / SSLC + ITI

Matriculation / SSLC plus ITI from recognised NCVT / SCVT institutions in the prescribed trades including Fitter, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic, Mechanic (Radio & TV), Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Wireman, Tractor Mechanic, Armature & Coil Winder, Mechanic (Diesel), Heat Engine, Turner, Machinist and Refrigeration & Air-Conditioning Mechanic.

Route B — Course Completed Act Apprenticeship

Matriculation / SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in the prescribed trades mentioned in the notification.

Route C — Diploma / Degree in Engineering

Matriculation / SSLC plus a three-year Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile Engineering, or combinations of these engineering disciplines, from a recognised institution in lieu of ITI. A Degree in the relevant engineering disciplines is also acceptable in lieu of the Diploma.

Important Note

Candidates must already possess the prescribed educational / technical qualification at the time of submitting the online application. Candidates awaiting final results of the prescribed qualification are not eligible to apply.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Tibetan refugee who came to India before 01.01.1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Person of Indian origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India, subject to the conditions prescribed in the notification.
  • Where a Certificate of Eligibility is required, it must be issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 30 Years

The age of candidates should be between 18 and 30 years as on 01-07-2026, subject to the applicable age-relaxation rules given in CEN No. 01/2026.

For UR / OBC-CL and EWS candidates, the upper age date is prescribed in the notification. Separate relaxations are available to eligible reserved and other specified categories as per the CEN.

Medical Requirements

Assistant Loco Pilot carries the A-1 medical standard.

Medical Standard Requirement
A-1 Physically fit in all respects with prescribed distant and near vision requirements and required colour, binocular, field, night and mesopic vision tests.
Distant Vision 6/6, 6/6 without glasses with fogging test as prescribed in the notification.
Near Vision Sn 0.6, 0.6 without glasses.
Vision Tests Colour Vision, Binocular Vision, Field of Vision, Night Vision and Mesopic Vision tests are required.

Medical Examination

  • The A-1 medical standard is mandatory for the ALP post.
  • Candidates must satisfy the prescribed vision and physical / medical standards.
  • Candidates who have undergone LASIK surgery or another surgical procedure to correct refractive error are not eligible for ALP as stated in the notification.
  • Self-screening medical declarations and reports are required for candidates shortlisted for CBAT as prescribed.
  • Final medical fitness is determined by the Railway Medical Authorities according to the applicable Railway medical standards.
  • Candidates found medically unsuitable for ALP shall not be given an alternative appointment.

Examination Pattern

RRB ALP CEN No. 01/2026 recruitment comprises CBT-1, CBT-2, CBAT, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

CBT-1 is a screening examination used for shortlisting candidates for CBT-2. Its marks are not counted while preparing the final panel.

CBT-1 Syllabus

Subjects and broad topics prescribed in CEN No. 01/2026.

Subject Broad Topics
Mathematics Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar, Clock, Pipes & Cisterns.
Mental Ability Analogies, Alphabetical and Number Series, Coding and Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation and Sufficiency, Conclusions and Decision Making, Similarities and Differences, Analytical Reasoning, Classification, Directions, Statement–Arguments and Assumptions.
General Science Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences of 10th standard level.
Stage - 2

Second Stage CBT (CBT-2)

CBT-2 consists of Part-A and Part-B. Part-A marks are used for further shortlisting, while Part-B is a qualifying test.

Total Duration 2 Hours 30 Minutes
Total Questions 175
Part-A Duration 90 Minutes
Part-A Questions 100
Part-B Duration 60 Minutes
Part-B Questions 75
Negative Marking 1/3 mark for every wrong answer
Part-B Qualifying Percentage 35% for all candidates irrespective of category
Part-A Minimum UR & EWS – 40%, OBC (NCL) – 30%, SC – 30%, ST – 25%

CBT-2 Part-A

Only Part-A marks are counted for shortlisting to subsequent stages, provided Part-B is qualified.

Mathematics

Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics and other topics prescribed in the CEN.

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Series, Coding-Decoding, Analogy, Syllogism, Classification, Directions, Analytical Reasoning and related topics.

Basic Science & Engineering

Engineering Drawing, Units, Measurements, Work, Power, Energy, Speed, Heat, Electricity, Machines, Safety, Environment and IT Literacy.

CBT-2 Part-B

  • Part-B is qualifying in nature.
  • Qualifying percentage is 35% for all candidates irrespective of category / community.
  • ITI / Trade Apprenticeship candidates answer questions from their respective trade.
  • Degree / Diploma candidates select a trade from the notified grouping corresponding to their engineering discipline.
  • Part-B trade syllabus is based on the trade syllabi prescribed by the Directorate General of Training (DGT).

CBT-2 Part-B Trade Grouping

Qualification-wise trade / subject choices prescribed in the notification.

Electrical Engineering

Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Wireman, Armature & Coil Winder, Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning Mechanic.

Electronics Engineering

Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic (Radio & TV).

Mechanical Engineering

Fitter, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel), Turner, Machinist, Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning Mechanic, Heat Engine, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic.

Automobile Engineering

Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel), Heat Engine, Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning Mechanic.

Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT)

CBAT is a mandatory stage for candidates shortlisted for ALP.

Shortlisting

Candidates equal to 8 times the ALP vacancies for each notified community / category are shortlisted, subject to qualifying Part-B.

Language

CBAT is conducted only in English and Hindi.

No Negative Marking

There is no negative marking in the Computer Based Aptitude Test.

Qualifying Score

A minimum T-score of 42 marks is required in each test battery separately.

CBAT Important Points

Point Details
Shortlisting Ratio Up to 8 times the number of ALP vacancies for each notified community / category, subject to the conditions in the CEN.
Part-B Requirement Candidate must qualify Part-B of CBT-2 before being considered for CBAT.
Test Batteries Each test battery / section of CBAT must be cleared separately.
Minimum T-score 42 marks in each test battery.
Negative Marking None.
Medical Declaration Self Screening Medical Declaration Report in the prescribed format must be produced in original on CBAT day as required.

Recruitment Process

CBT-1

First-stage screening examination for shortlisting candidates for CBT-2.

CBT-2

Two-part examination consisting of Part-A and qualifying Part-B.

CBAT

Computer Based Aptitude Test for candidates shortlisted for ALP.

Document Verification

Original certificates and required documents are verified.

Medical Examination

Final medical fitness is assessed according to the prescribed A-1 standard.

Final Merit

  • The merit list is prepared RRB-wise from candidates who qualify in CBAT.
  • Part-A of CBT-2 carries 50% weightage in the final merit calculation.
  • CBAT scores carry 50% weightage in the final merit calculation.
  • Part-B of CBT-2 must be qualified, but its marks are not counted for the final merit weightage.
  • Candidates equal to the number of vacancies are shortlisted for Document Verification and Medical Examination based on combined merit, preferences and applicable conditions.
  • Final appointment is subject to medical fitness, document verification and verification of antecedents / character.

Application Fee Details

Modification Fee ₹250 per occasion
General Fee ₹500; ₹400 is refundable after appearing in CBT-1, after applicable bank charges.
Concessional Fee ₹250 for SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, Female, Transgender, Minority and EBC candidates; refundable after appearing in CBT-1, after applicable bank charges.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Read Official Notification thoroughly before applying.
  • A candidate can apply to only one RRB and only one online application is accepted for this CEN.
  • The selected RRB cannot be changed after account creation / submission as specified in the notification.
  • Keep an active personal mobile number and valid personal email ID throughout the recruitment process.
  • Negative marking of 1/3 mark applies in CBT-1 and CBT-2.
  • There is no negative marking in CBAT.
  • Do not carry prohibited electronic gadgets, calculators, books, bags and other banned items into the examination centre.
  • Use only official RRB websites for examination schedules, e-call letters and recruitment updates.
  • False information, multiple applications or unfair practices can lead to rejection and debarment.

  • Educational and technical qualifications must already be obtained by the closing date of application.
  • Details entered during account creation, including mobile number and email ID, cannot be modified later.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB ALP?

RRB ALP stands for Assistant Loco Pilot. CEN No. 01/2026 is the recruitment notice for the Assistant Loco Pilot post.

How many ALP vacancies are notified in CEN 01/2026?

A total of 11,127 Assistant Loco Pilot vacancies are notified across all RRBs.

What is the age limit for RRB ALP 2026?

The prescribed age is 18–30 years as on 01-07-2026, subject to applicable age-relaxation rules.

What is the initial basic pay of ALP?

The initial pay is ₹19,900 under Level-2 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix.

What is the educational qualification for ALP?

Candidates can qualify through the prescribed Matriculation / SSLC plus specified ITI trades or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship, or through the prescribed three-year engineering Diploma / relevant engineering Degree route.

What are the stages of selection?

The recruitment process comprises CBT-1, CBT-2, CBAT, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

How many questions are in CBT-1?

CBT-1 has 75 objective questions carrying 75 marks, with a duration of 60 minutes.

What is the pattern of CBT-2?

CBT-2 has 175 questions and a total duration of 2 hours 30 minutes. Part-A has 100 questions in 90 minutes and Part-B has 75 questions in 60 minutes.

Is Part-B of CBT-2 counted in final merit?

Part-B is qualifying in nature. Part-A marks are used for subsequent merit / shortlisting provided the candidate qualifies Part-B.

Is CBAT compulsory for ALP?

Candidates shortlisted for ALP must qualify the Computer Based Aptitude Test before final merit and Document Verification.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for each wrong answer in CBT-1 and CBT-2. There is no negative marking in CBAT.

What is the medical standard for ALP?

The notified medical standard for Assistant Loco Pilot is A-1, with stringent vision and other medical requirements.

Can a candidate with LASIK apply for ALP?

The CEN states that candidates who have undergone LASIK surgery or another surgical procedure to correct refractive error are not eligible for the ALP post.
