Posts Offered

RRB Technician recruitment includes Technician Grade-I Signal and various Technician Grade-III categories.

Technician Grade-I Signal

Pay Level-5. Initial basic pay ₹29,200. This is the higher-level Technician post with prescribed technical qualification and B-1 medical standard.

Technician Grade-III

Pay Level-2. Initial basic pay ₹19,900. Recruitment covers multiple technical categories across Railway departments.

Track Machine

One of the notified Technician Grade-III categories involving technical work related to Railway track machines.

Electrical Categories

Grade-III categories include Electrical/TRS, Electrical (GS), Electrical (TRD), EMU and other notified electrical posts.

Mechanical Categories

Grade-III categories include Diesel Mechanical, Fitter, Carriage & Wagon, Machinist, Millwright and other notified technical posts.

Workshop & Other Trades

Notified categories also include Welder, Carpenter, Painter, Trimmer, Crane Driver and other technical workshop posts.

Job Profile

Technician Grade-I Signal

  • Technical work in Signal & Telecommunication department
  • Maintenance and inspection of signalling equipment
  • Work involving electrical, electronic and communication systems
  • Fault identification and technical troubleshooting
  • Safety-related technical duties as prescribed by Railway

Technician Grade-III

  • Technical maintenance and repair work
  • Operation and maintenance of Railway equipment
  • Department-specific technical duties
  • Inspection and troubleshooting
  • Workshop, electrical, mechanical or engineering duties according to allotted category

Electrical Technician

  • Electrical equipment maintenance
  • Inspection and fault detection
  • Maintenance of electrical systems
  • Safety procedures
  • Technical documentation and reporting

Mechanical Technician

  • Mechanical equipment maintenance
  • Repair and servicing
  • Inspection of machines and components
  • Workshop-related technical duties
  • Following Railway safety procedures

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Qualification depends on the post. Grade-I Signal requires prescribed B.Sc., Diploma or Engineering qualification, while Grade-III posts generally require Matriculation/SSLC plus relevant ITI or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship.

Nationality

Indian citizens and other eligible categories specified under the RRB rules may apply, subject to the prescribed conditions.

Age

18–33 Years for Technician Grade-I Signal
18–30 Years for Technician Grade-III

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed Railway medical standard for the post opted for.

Educational Qualification

Technician Grade-I Signal

Candidates must possess one of the prescribed qualifications in Physics, Electronics, Computer Science, Information Technology or Instrumentation, such as a B.Sc. degree, a three-year Diploma in Engineering, or an Engineering Degree in the prescribed streams or combinations.

Technician Grade-III

Candidates must generally possess Matriculation / SSLC plus ITI from a recognized NCVT/SCVT institution in the prescribed trade, or Matriculation / SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in the relevant trade.

Important Qualification Rule

The prescribed educational or technical qualification must already have been obtained from a recognized institution. Candidates awaiting the final result of the prescribed qualification are not eligible. For Pay Level-2 Technician posts, a Diploma or Degree in Engineering is not accepted in lieu of ITI / Course Completed Act Apprenticeship unless specifically prescribed.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Tibetan refugee who came to India before the prescribed cut-off date with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Person of Indian Origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India, subject to the conditions prescribed by the Government of India.
  • Where a Certificate of Eligibility is required, it must be issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 33 Years / 18 – 30 Years

The prescribed age limit is 18–33 years for Technician Grade-I Signal and 18–30 years for Technician Grade-III.

Age relaxation is available to eligible reserved categories and other specified categories according to the applicable Government rules.

Important Highlights

Online Exam

The Technician CBT is conducted in computer-based mode.

100 Questions

Each CBT contains 100 objective-type questions.

90 Minutes

The standard examination duration is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Questions are objective-type Multiple Choice Questions.
  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark is deducted.
  • There is no negative marking for an unanswered question.
  • Normalization of marks is carried out for CBTs held in multiple shifts.
  • The CBT marks are used for shortlisting candidates for further stages.

Selection Process

Computer Based Test

A separate CBT is conducted for each Pay Level. Candidates are shortlisted on the basis of merit in the respective CBT.

Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates must produce original educational, age, category and other required documents.

Medical Examination

Selected candidates must pass the prescribed Railway medical fitness standard for the opted post.

Post Specific Requirements

Medical standards and educational qualifications vary according to the Technician category.

Post / CategoryPay LevelKey Requirement
Technician Grade-I SignalLevel-5Prescribed B.Sc. / Diploma / Engineering qualification in relevant streams
Technician Grade-IIILevel-2Matriculation / SSLC + prescribed ITI or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship
Technician Grade-III Track MachineLevel-2Relevant ITI / Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in prescribed trades
Other Grade-III CategoriesLevel-2Qualification and medical standard as prescribed for the particular category

Medical Examination

  • Candidates shortlisted for Document Verification must pass the requisite Railway medical fitness test.
  • Visual acuity is an important part of Railway medical fitness.
  • Different Technician categories can have different medical standards such as A-3, B-1, B-2 and C-1.
  • A candidate found medically unsuitable for the opted post will not be given an alternative appointment.
  • The exact medical requirements should be checked for the particular Technician category before applying.

Reservation & Relaxation

Reservation and applicable concessions are available to eligible categories such as SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen according to the applicable Government rules and the conditions prescribed in the RRB notification.

Candidates claiming reservation, fee concession or age relaxation must possess the required certificates in the prescribed format and produce them during Document Verification.

Language of Examination

CBT Languages

Questions are available in English, Hindi and 13 regional Indian languages listed by RRB.

Question Paper

Regional language options include Bengali, Assamese, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Read the complete RRB Technician notification carefully before applying.
  • Check the post-wise educational qualification and medical standard before selecting a post.
  • A separate application and examination fee are required for each Pay Level.
  • Only one RRB can be selected for a particular Pay Level.
  • Keep your registered mobile number and personal email ID active throughout the recruitment process.
  • Do not carry prohibited electronic devices or other banned items to the examination centre.
  • Carry the required e-call letter and valid original photo identity proof to the CBT.
  • Follow only official RRB websites for recruitment updates and examination information.

Important Instructions

  • Educational and technical qualifications must be completed as prescribed for the relevant post.
  • Candidates awaiting final results of the prescribed qualification are not eligible.
  • Eligibility is initially provisional and is verified against original documents during Document Verification.
  • False information, forged documents, impersonation or unfair means can lead to cancellation and debarment.
  • Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standards for their selected post.
  • RRBs do not appoint agents or coaching centres for recruitment. Selection is based on merit and the prescribed recruitment process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB Technician?

RRB Technician is a Railway Recruitment Board examination for Technician Grade-I Signal and various Technician Grade-III categories in Indian Railways.

What is the selection process?

The recruitment process comprises Computer Based Test (CBT), Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination (ME).

What is the age limit?

The age limit is 18–33 years for Technician Grade-I Signal and 18–30 years for Technician Grade-III, with applicable relaxation according to Government rules.

What is the qualification for Technician Grade-I Signal?

Eligible candidates may possess the prescribed B.Sc. qualification, a three-year Diploma in Engineering or an Engineering Degree in the relevant streams specified in the notification.

What is the qualification for Technician Grade-III?

Technician Grade-III posts generally require Matriculation / SSLC plus prescribed ITI or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in the relevant trade.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer in the CBT.

How many questions are asked?

Both Technician Grade-I Signal and Technician Grade-III CBTs contain 100 objective-type questions with a standard duration of 90 minutes.

What is the application fee?

The prescribed fee is ₹500 for candidates in the general fee category and ₹250 for SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD, Female, Transgender, Minority and EBC candidates. Applicable refund is made after appearing in CBT, after bank charges.

Is a separate CBT conducted for each Pay Level?

Yes. A separate CBT is conducted for each Pay Level, and the applicable examination fee is also paid separately for each Pay Level.

Is medical examination compulsory?

Yes. Candidates selected for further stages must satisfy the prescribed Railway medical standard for the relevant Technician post.
