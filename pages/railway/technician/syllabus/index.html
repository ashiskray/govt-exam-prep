RRB Technician Syllabus

Explore the complete RRB Technician syllabus for Technician Grade-I Signal and Technician Grade-III. The syllabus follows the official CEN No. 02/2026 notification and covers the CBT subjects, topic-wise areas and indicative question distribution for both pay levels.

CBT Highlights

100 Questions

Both Grade-I Signal and Grade-III CBTs contain 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks.

90 Minutes

The standard CBT duration is 90 minutes. Eligible candidates may receive compensatory time as prescribed.

1/3 Negative Marking

One-third mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the CBT.

Normalization

Normalization of marks is done for CBTs conducted in multiple shifts.

Selection Process

The recruitment process comprises Computer Based Test, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage 1 — CBT

A separate CBT is conducted for each pay level. CBT marks are used to shortlist candidates for the further stages.

Stage 2 — Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates must produce original certificates and documents for verification.

Stage 3 — Medical Examination

Appointment is subject to passing the requisite Railway medical fitness test and final verification.

Minimum Qualifying Marks

Minimum percentages specified in the official notification for CBT eligibility.

CategoryMinimum Percentage

  • The same minimum percentage framework applies to Ex-Servicemen according to their community.
  • The prescribed percentage may be relaxed by 2 marks for PwBD candidates in case of shortage of PwBD candidates against reserved vacancies.
  • These are minimum eligibility percentages and should not be treated as the expected cut-off.

Important Syllabus Notes

  • There is a separate CBT for Pay Level-5 Technician Grade-I Signal and Pay Level-2 Technician Grade-III.
  • Both CBTs contain 100 objective-type questions and have a standard duration of 90 minutes.
  • There is 1/3 negative marking for every wrong answer.
  • The subject-wise question distribution is indicative; the actual question paper may vary.
  • Grade-I Signal includes computer applications and Basic Science & Engineering in addition to General Awareness, Reasoning and Mathematics.
  • Grade-III includes Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science and General Awareness.
  • The topics listed in the official notification are illustrative and not necessarily exhaustive.
  • CBT marks are used for shortlisting candidates for the subsequent stages.

How to Prepare According to the Syllabus

Mathematics

Build strong fundamentals in the notified arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics and probability topics applicable to your category.

Reasoning

Practise series, coding-decoding, analogy, classification, directions, syllogism, Venn diagrams, data interpretation and analytical reasoning.

Science

For Grade-III, revise Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at 10th standard level. Grade-I candidates should additionally master the notified engineering areas.

Computer & Engineering

Grade-I candidates should give dedicated time to computer fundamentals, networking, operating systems, MS Office, electricity, magnetism and electronics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are asked in RRB Technician CBT?

Both Technician Grade-I Signal and Grade-III CBTs contain 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks.

What is the duration of the RRB Technician CBT?

The standard CBT duration is 90 minutes.

Is there negative marking in RRB Technician?

Yes. One-third mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the CBT.

What is the syllabus of Technician Grade-I Signal?

Grade-I Signal covers General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Basics of Computers and Applications, Mathematics, and Basic Science & Engineering.

What is the syllabus of Technician Grade-III?

Grade-III covers Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science and General Awareness. General Science is at 10th standard level.

How many Mathematics questions are asked in Grade-I Signal?

The tentative distribution gives 20 Mathematics questions carrying 20 marks. The notification states that the section-wise distribution is indicative and may vary.

How many General Science questions are asked in Grade-III?

The tentative distribution gives 40 General Science questions carrying 40 marks. The notification states that the section-wise distribution is indicative and may vary.

Is the Technician CBT conducted separately for Grade-I and Grade-III?

Yes. The notification provides a separate CBT for each pay level: Level-5 for Grade-I Signal and Level-2 for Grade-III.

What are the minimum qualifying marks in Technician CBT?

The notification specifies 40% for UR and EWS, 30% for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and SC, and 25% for ST candidates, subject to the specified PwBD relaxation.

Does the official notification list the complete trade-wise technical syllabus?

The common CBT syllabus for Grade-III is given in the notification. Post-wise technical qualification and trade requirements are provided separately in the post-parameter section.
