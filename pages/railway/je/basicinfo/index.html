Posts & Engineering Disciplines

RRB JE includes technical posts across major Railway engineering departments.

Junior Engineer — Electrical

Includes electrical posts related to general services, EMU & TRS, TRD, design, production, R&D and workshops.

Junior Engineer — Civil

Includes Civil P-Way & Bridge, Design Drawing & Estimation, Works & Research, Track Machine and other Civil categories.

Junior Engineer — Mechanical

Includes Carriage & Wagon, Diesel Mechanical, Mechanical Power, Production, Design, Workshop and R&D categories.

Junior Engineer — S&T

Includes Signal, Telecommunication, Design Drawing & Estimation, Workshop and related technical categories.

Electronics & Allied

Relevant qualifications may be mapped to Electronics and Allied Engineering exam groups, including instrumentation, communication and computer-related disciplines.

Other Notified Categories

The CEN also includes categories such as Depot Material Superintendent and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant; eligibility is post-specific.

Job Profile

Civil JE

  • Technical supervision and engineering work in Civil departments
  • Work related to track, bridges, works, design and estimation
  • Inspection, maintenance and technical reporting
  • Execution and supervision of engineering activities
  • Railway safety and departmental technical duties

Electrical JE

  • Technical work in electrical departments
  • Maintenance and inspection of electrical systems and equipment
  • Work in areas such as EMU, TRS, TRD, general services and workshops
  • Fault identification and technical troubleshooting
  • Safety-related electrical duties

Mechanical JE

  • Technical supervision of Railway mechanical equipment
  • Maintenance, inspection and repair-related work
  • Work in Carriage & Wagon, Diesel, Power, Production and workshops
  • Technical testing and troubleshooting
  • Department-specific engineering duties

S&T JE

  • Technical work related to Signal and Telecommunication systems
  • Inspection and maintenance of signalling equipment
  • Telecommunication and workshop-related technical duties
  • Fault detection and technical troubleshooting
  • Railway safety and communication-system responsibilities

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Candidates must possess the prescribed educational or technical qualification for the selected JE post and corresponding exam group.

Nationality

Indian citizens and other eligible categories specified by the Government of India may apply, subject to the prescribed conditions.

Age

The prescribed normal age range for JE is 18–33 years, with applicable relaxations for eligible categories.

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the medical standard prescribed for the particular post. Medical standards vary by category.

Educational Qualification

Junior Engineer — General Requirement

The prescribed qualification is post-specific. For many JE categories, a three-year Diploma in the relevant Engineering discipline or a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering/Technology in the prescribed discipline is accepted.

Major Engineering Groups

The notification maps qualifications to technical exam groups including Mechanical and Allied Engineering, Electrical and Allied Engineering, Electronics and Allied Engineering, and Civil and Allied Engineering.

Examples of Relevant Qualifications

Mechanical-related qualifications include Mechanical, Production, Automobile, Manufacturing, Mechatronics, Industrial, Machining, Tools & Machining and Tools & Die Making Engineering. Electrical-related qualifications include Electrical Engineering and specified combinations. Electronics-related qualifications include Electronics, Instrumentation & Control, Communication, Computer Science, Computer Engineering and Information Technology. Civil JE categories require the prescribed Civil Engineering qualification.

Important Qualification Rule

The prescribed educational or technical qualification must already have been obtained from a recognized University, Institute or Board as applicable. Candidates awaiting the final result of the prescribed qualification are not eligible.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Citizen of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Eligible persons of Indian origin who have migrated from the countries specified in the notification with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Where a Certificate of Eligibility is required, it must be issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 33 Years

The normal prescribed age range for Junior Engineer posts is 18 to 33 years.

Age relaxation is available to eligible categories such as SC, ST, OBC-NCL, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other categories according to the applicable Government rules.

Examination Pattern

RRB JE recruitment comprises two stages of Computer Based Test, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. CBT-I is common for all notified posts, while CBT-II contains a Technical Abilities section based on the candidate's mapped exam group.

CBT-II is the main examination and includes common sections together with Technical Abilities for the relevant JE exam group.

Important Highlights

Online Exam

Both stages of the examination are conducted in computer-based mode.

100 Questions in CBT-I

The first-stage CBT contains 100 objective-type questions.

90 Minutes

The standard duration of CBT-I is 90 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark of the allotted mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Questions are objective-type Multiple Choice Questions.
  • Each correct answer carries the allotted mark.
  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark of the allotted mark is deducted.
  • There is no negative marking for an unanswered question.
  • Marks may be normalized where CBTs are conducted in multiple shifts.
  • CBT-I marks are used for shortlisting candidates for CBT-II.

Selection Process

CBT-I

The common first-stage CBT is used for screening and shortlisting candidates for CBT-II.

CBT-II

The second-stage CBT includes common subjects and 100 questions on Technical Abilities.

Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates must produce original educational, age, category and other required documents.

Medical Examination

Selected candidates must pass the prescribed Railway medical fitness standard for the opted post.

Technical Abilities & Exam Groups

Technical questions in CBT-II are based on the exam group mapped to the candidate's prescribed educational qualification.

Major Exam Group Relevant Engineering Backgrounds
Mechanical & Allied Engineering Mechanical, Production, Automobile, Manufacturing, Mechatronics, Industrial, Machining, Tools & Machining, Tools & Die Making and specified combinations
Electrical & Allied Engineering Electrical Engineering and specified combinations of Electrical Engineering
Electronics & Allied Engineering Electronics, Instrumentation & Control, Communication, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Technology and specified combinations
Civil & Allied Engineering Civil Engineering and specified combinations of Civil Engineering

The detailed Technical Abilities syllabus is defined through the respective exam-group syllabus in the notification. Candidates should prepare the technical subjects corresponding to the qualification and post group for which they are eligible.

Medical Examination

  • Candidates shortlisted for Document Verification must pass the requisite Railway medical fitness test.
  • Visual acuity is an important component of Railway medical fitness.
  • Different JE categories can have different medical standards such as A-3, B-1, B-2 and C-1.
  • Candidates found medically unsuitable for the opted post may not be given an alternative appointment, subject to the applicable rules.
  • Candidates should check the medical standard prescribed for their particular JE category before applying.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Read the complete RRB JE notification carefully before applying.
  • Check the post-wise educational qualification and medical standard before selecting a post.
  • A candidate can apply to only one RRB as prescribed in the notification.
  • Only one common online application is to be submitted for the eligible notified posts under the selected RRB.
  • Keep the registered mobile number and personal email ID active throughout the recruitment process.
  • Do not carry prohibited electronic devices or banned items to the examination centre.
  • Carry the required e-call letter and valid original photo identity proof to the CBT.
  • Follow official RRB websites for examination-related information.

  • Educational and technical qualifications must satisfy the requirements of the selected post and exam group.
  • Candidates waiting for the final result of the prescribed qualification should not apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB JE?

RRB JE is a Railway Recruitment Board examination for Junior Engineer posts in different technical departments of Indian Railways.

What is the selection process?

The process comprises CBT-I, CBT-II, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

How many CBT stages are there?

There are two Computer Based Test stages: CBT-I and CBT-II.

How many questions are asked in CBT-I?

CBT-I contains 100 questions for 100 marks and has a standard duration of 90 minutes.

How many questions are asked in CBT-II?

CBT-II contains 150 questions for 150 marks and has a standard duration of 120 minutes.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark of the allotted mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

What qualification is required?

The qualification is post-specific and generally includes a relevant three-year Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Engineering/Technology as prescribed for the post.

What is the initial basic pay?

Junior Engineer posts are in Pay Level 6 with an initial basic pay of ₹35,400.

What is the application fee?

The prescribed fee is ₹500 for the general fee category and ₹250 for SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, Female, Transgender, Minority and EBC candidates, with applicable refund after appearing in CBT-I.
