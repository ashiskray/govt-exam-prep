RRB JE CBT Exam Pattern

The RRB JE recruitment process consists of two stages of Computer Based Tests followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage Subject Questions Duration
CBT-I Mathematics 30 90 Minutes
CBT-I General Intelligence & Reasoning 25
CBT-I General Awareness 15
CBT-I General Science 30
CBT-I Total 100 90 Minutes
CBT-II General Awareness 15 120 Minutes
CBT-II Physics & Chemistry 15
CBT-II Basics of Computers & Applications 10
CBT-II Basics of Environment & Pollution Control 10
CBT-II Technical Abilities 100
CBT-II Total 150 120 Minutes

CBT Highlights

CBT-I — 100 Questions

CBT-I contains 100 objective-type questions covering Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness and General Science.

CBT-II — 150 Questions

CBT-II contains 150 questions covering General Awareness, Physics & Chemistry, Computer Applications, Environment & Pollution Control and Technical Abilities.

1/3 Negative Marking

One-third mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the CBT examinations.

Virtual Calculator

A virtual calculator is made available on the computer monitor during the second stage CBT.

Selection Process

The RRB JE recruitment process consists of two stages of Computer Based Tests followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Stage 1 — CBT-I

The first-stage CBT is a screening examination used for shortlisting candidates for the second-stage CBT.

Stage 2 — CBT-II

The second-stage CBT contains common subjects along with Technical Abilities according to the candidate's applicable exam group.

Stage 3 — Document Verification

Candidates shortlisted after the CBT stages are called for verification of their original educational and other required documents.

Stage 4 — Medical Examination

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standards applicable to the post for which they are being considered.

RRB JE Technical Exam Groups

The Technical Abilities section is based on the engineering discipline mapped to the candidate's qualification.

Engineering Qualification Exam Group
Civil Engineering Civil & Allied Engineering
Electrical Engineering Electrical & Allied Engineering
Electronics Engineering, Instrumentation & Control, Communication, Computer Science / IT related qualifications Electronics & Allied Engineering
Mechanical Engineering and mapped allied engineering disciplines Mechanical & Allied Engineering

Important Syllabus Notes

  • CBT-I is a screening examination used for shortlisting candidates for CBT-II.
  • CBT-I consists of 100 questions carrying 100 marks and has a duration of 90 minutes.
  • CBT-II consists of 150 questions carrying 150 marks and has a duration of 120 minutes.
  • The section-wise distribution of questions in the examination is indicative and may vary in the actual question paper.
  • There is negative marking of one-third mark for every wrong answer in the CBT.
  • The CBT-II Technical Abilities section contains 100 questions and is based on the applicable RRB JE Exam Group.
  • The official notification defines separate Technical Abilities syllabi for Civil & Allied, Electrical & Allied, Electronics & Allied and Mechanical & Allied Engineering groups.
  • Candidates should prepare the Technical Abilities syllabus corresponding to the engineering discipline mapped to their qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RRB JE CBT-I syllabus?

CBT-I covers Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness and General Science. Mathematics includes Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock and Pipes & Cistern. General Intelligence & Reasoning includes analogies, alphabetical and number series, coding-decoding, mathematical operations, relationships, syllogism, jumbling, Venn diagrams, data interpretation and sufficiency, conclusions and decision making, similarities and differences, analytical reasoning, classification, directions, statement-arguments and assumptions. General Awareness includes current affairs, Indian geography, culture, history including freedom struggle, polity and constitution, economy, environmental issues, sports and scientific and technological developments. General Science covers Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences up to 10th-standard CBSE level.

What is the RRB JE CBT-II syllabus?

CBT-II covers General Awareness, Physics & Chemistry, Basics of Computers and Applications, Basics of Environment and Pollution Control and Technical Abilities.

What is included in RRB JE Computer syllabus?

The Computer syllabus includes Computer Architecture, Input and Output Devices, Storage Devices, Networking, Operating Systems such as Windows, Unix and Linux, MS Office, data representation, Internet and Email, Websites and Web Browsers and Computer Virus.

What is included in Environment and Pollution Control?

The syllabus includes Basics of Environment, adverse effects of environmental pollution and control strategies, Air, Water and Noise Pollution, their effects and control, Waste Management, Global Warming, Acid Rain and Ozone Depletion.

How many questions are there in RRB JE CBT-II?

CBT-II contains 150 objective-type questions. General Awareness has 15 questions, Physics & Chemistry 15 questions, Basics of Computers and Applications 10 questions, Basics of Environment and Pollution Control 10 questions and Technical Abilities 100 questions.

What is the Technical Abilities syllabus in RRB JE?

Technical Abilities are based on the applicable RRB JE Exam Group. The official notification provides separate syllabi for Civil & Allied Engineering, Electrical & Allied Engineering, Electronics & Allied Engineering and Mechanical & Allied Engineering.

What is the Civil Engineering syllabus for RRB JE?

The Civil & Allied Engineering syllabus includes Engineering Mechanics, Building Construction, Building Materials, Construction of Substructure, Construction of Superstructure, Building Finishes, Building Maintenance, Building Drawing, Concrete Technology, Surveying, Computer Aided Design, Geo Technical Engineering, Hydraulics, Irrigation Engineering, Mechanics of Structures, Theory of Structures, Design of Concrete Structures, Design of Steel Structures, Transportation Engineering, Highway Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Advanced Construction Techniques and Equipment, Estimating and Costing and Contracts and Accounts.

What is the Electrical Engineering syllabus for RRB JE?

The Electrical & Allied Engineering syllabus includes Basic Concepts, Circuit Law, Magnetic Circuit, AC Fundamentals, Measurement and Measuring Instruments, Electrical Machines, Synchronous Machines, Generation Transmission and Distribution, Estimation and Costing, Utilization of Electrical Energy and Basic Electronics.

What is the Electronics Engineering syllabus for RRB JE?

The Electronics & Allied Engineering syllabus includes Electronic Components and Materials, Electronic Devices and Circuits, Digital Electronics, Linear Integrated Circuits, Microprocessor and Microcontroller, Electronic Measurements, Communication Engineering, Data Communication and Network, Computer Programming and Basic Electrical Engineering.

What is the Mechanical Engineering syllabus for RRB JE?

The Mechanical & Allied Engineering syllabus includes Engineering Mechanics, Material Science, Strength of Materials, Machining, Welding, Grinding and Finishing Process, Metrology, Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulic Machinery, Industrial Management and Thermal Engineering.

Is there negative marking in RRB JE?

Yes. The official notification provides negative marking of one-third mark for every wrong answer in the CBT.
