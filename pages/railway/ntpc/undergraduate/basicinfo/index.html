Posts Offered

RRB NTPC Under Graduate Level CEN No. 07/2025 includes the following four posts.

Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk

Pay Level-3. Initial Pay ₹21,700. Medical Standard B-2.

Accounts Clerk Cum Typist

Pay Level-2. Initial Pay ₹19,900. Medical Standard C-2. Computer typing proficiency is essential.

Junior Clerk Cum Typist

Pay Level-2. Initial Pay ₹19,900. Medical Standard C-2. Computer typing proficiency is essential.

Trains Clerk

Pay Level-2. Initial Pay ₹19,900. Medical Standard A-3.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent from a recognized Board/University/Institute as prescribed.

Nationality

Indian citizen or other eligible categories specified in the official notification.

Age

18–30 Years
(As on 01-01-2026)

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the medical standards prescribed for the particular post.

Educational Qualification

Under Graduate Level Posts

Candidates must already possess the minimum educational qualification prescribed in CEN No. 07/2025 on or before the closing date of application.

Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent with not less than 50% marks in aggregate. The 50% requirement is not insisted upon for SC/ST, Persons with Benchmark Disability, Ex-Servicemen and candidates possessing qualifications higher than 12th (+2 Stage).

Accounts Clerk Cum Typist

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent with not less than 50% marks in aggregate, subject to the exemptions specified in the notification. Typing proficiency in English or Hindi on Computer is essential.

Junior Clerk Cum Typist

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent with not less than 50% marks in aggregate, subject to the exemptions specified in the notification. Typing proficiency in English or Hindi on Computer is essential.

Trains Clerk

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent with not less than 50% marks in aggregate. The 50% requirement is not insisted upon for SC/ST, Persons with Benchmark Disability, Ex-Servicemen and candidates possessing qualifications higher than 12th (+2 Stage).

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Tibetan refugee who came to India before 01.01.1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Person of Indian Origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India, subject to the conditions in the notification.
  • Where a Certificate of Eligibility is required, it must be issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 30 Years

The age of candidates should be between 18 and 30 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to the post parameters and age-relaxation rules given in CEN No. 07/2025.

Age relaxation is available to eligible reserved categories and other specified categories as per the rules given in the notification.

Examination Pattern

RRB NTPC Under Graduate recruitment is conducted through CBT-I and CBT-II, followed by the Computer Based Typing Skill Test for the applicable posts, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Important Highlights

Online Exam

CBT-I is conducted in computer-based mode.

100 Questions

There are 100 objective questions in CBT-I.

90 Minutes

Standard duration is 90 minutes. Candidates eligible to use a scribe get 120 minutes.

Negative Marking

1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark is deducted.
  • No marks are awarded or deducted for unanswered questions.
  • Questions are objective type Multiple Choice Questions.
  • The normalized score of CBT-I is used for shortlisting candidates for CBT-II as prescribed by RRB.

Post-Specific Skill Test

Computer Based Typing Skill Test

CBTST is applicable to Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist.

Qualifying Nature

Marks obtained in CBTST are not added for making merit.

Typing Requirement

30 WPM in English or 25 WPM in Hindi on a Personal Computer without editing tools and spell-check facility.

Post Specific Requirements

Skill-test requirements differ according to the post notified in CEN No. 07/2025.

Junior Clerk Cum TypistLevel-2Computer Based Typing Skill Test

Computer Based Typing Skill Test

  • Accounts Clerk Cum Typist: Candidates must qualify the Computer Based Typing Skill Test.
  • Junior Clerk Cum Typist: Candidates must qualify the Computer Based Typing Skill Test.
  • The typing skill test is qualifying in nature; marks obtained are not added for making merit.
  • Candidates should be able to type 30 words per minute in English or 25 words per minute in Hindi.
  • The test is conducted on a Personal Computer without editing tools and spell-check facility.
  • For Hindi typing, Kruti Dev and Mangal fonts are made available as specified in the notification.
  • Eligible PwBD candidates may be exempted from the typing skill test subject to the conditions and certificate requirements prescribed in the notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB NTPC Under Graduate Level?

It is the recruitment examination for various Non-Technical Popular Categories (Under Graduate) posts notified by the Railway Recruitment Boards.

What is the educational qualification?

12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent is required for the notified posts, with the post-wise conditions prescribed in CEN No. 07/2025.

What is the age limit?

The prescribed age is 18–30 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to applicable age-relaxation rules.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer in CBT-I and CBT-II.

Is there a typing test?

Yes. A Computer Based Typing Skill Test is applicable to Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist.

What is the typing speed required?

30 words per minute in English or 25 words per minute in Hindi on a Personal Computer without editing tools and spell-check facility.

How many vacancies are notified?

CEN No. 07/2025 notifies a total of 3,058 vacancies across the four Under Graduate posts.

What are the CBT-I and CBT-II question counts?

CBT-I has 100 questions: 40 General Awareness, 30 Mathematics and 30 General Intelligence & Reasoning. CBT-II has 120 questions: 50 General Awareness, 35 Mathematics and 35 General Intelligence & Reasoning.
