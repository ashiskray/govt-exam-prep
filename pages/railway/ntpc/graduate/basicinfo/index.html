Posts Offered

RRB NTPC Graduate Level CEN No. 06/2025 includes the following six posts.

Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor

Pay Level-6. Responsible for commercial and ticketing related duties in railway establishments.

Station Master

Pay Level-6. Responsible for station operations, train movement coordination and safety-related duties.

Goods Train Manager

Pay Level-5. Responsible for duties associated with goods train operations and train working.

Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist

Pay Level-5. Accounts and clerical work with prescribed typing proficiency.

Senior Clerk Cum Typist

Pay Level-5. Senior clerical duties with prescribed typing proficiency.

Traffic Assistant

Pay Level-4. Railway traffic and operational duties as prescribed for the post.

  • For every incorrect answer, 1/3 mark is deducted.

Post-Specific Skill Tests

CBAT

Computer Based Aptitude Test is applicable to Station Master candidates.

Typing Skill Test

Typing test is applicable to Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist and Senior Clerk Cum Typist.

Other Posts

Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, Goods Train Manager and Traffic Assistant do not have CBAT or typing test as the post-specific skill test.

CBAT & Typing Test

  • Station Master: Candidates shortlisted for Station Master have to qualify the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT).
  • CBAT has multiple test batteries and candidates must secure the prescribed minimum T-score in each battery as specified by RRB.
  • For Station Master, the final merit weightage is 70% CBT-II marks + 30% CBAT marks, as prescribed in the notification.
  • Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist: Typing proficiency of 30 words per minute in English or 25 words per minute in Hindi is required.
  • Senior Clerk Cum Typist: Typing proficiency of 30 words per minute in English or 25 words per minute in Hindi is required.
  • The typing skill test is qualifying in nature and is conducted on a computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB NTPC Graduate Level?

It is the recruitment examination for various Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts notified by the Railway Recruitment Boards.

What is the educational qualification?

A Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent is required for the notified graduate-level posts.

What is the age limit?

The prescribed age is 18–33 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to applicable age-relaxation rules.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 1/3 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer in CBT-I and CBT-II.

Is there a typing test?

Yes. A Computer Based Typing Skill Test is applicable to Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist and Senior Clerk Cum Typist.

Is CBAT required for Station Master?

Yes. Candidates shortlisted for Station Master have to qualify the Computer Based Aptitude Test.

What is the typing speed required?

For the applicable typist posts, the prescribed speed is 30 words per minute in English or 25 words per minute in Hindi.

How many vacancies are notified?

CEN No. 06/2025 notifies a total of 5,810 vacancies across the six graduate-level posts.
