Posts Offered

SSC MTS recruitment includes Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff posts and Havaldar posts in CBIC and CBN.

Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS)

Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff is a General Central Service Group 'C' Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial post in various Ministries, Departments, Offices and other Government bodies.

Havaldar

Havaldar is a General Central Service Group 'C' Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial post in the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN).

Job Profile

The nature of work depends upon the department and post to which the candidate is allocated.

Multi-Tasking Staff

  • General office support work
  • Assisting departmental staff
  • Handling routine office duties
  • Movement of files and documents
  • Maintaining records and office-related work
  • Other duties assigned by the department

Havaldar

  • Works in CBIC / CBN
  • Performs duties assigned by the concerned department
  • Departmental field and office-related duties
  • May involve physical duties depending upon assignment
  • Posting may be according to allotted Post-cum-State / UT / CCA

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Candidates must have passed the Matriculation Examination or equivalent from a recognized Board.

Nationality

Candidates must satisfy the nationality / citizenship requirements prescribed by SSC.

Age

18 to 25 years for MTS.

18 to 27 years for Havaldar and certain MTS posts.

Physical Standards

PET / PST is applicable to candidates appearing for the Havaldar post.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed the Matriculation Examination or equivalent from a recognized Board on or before the cut-off date specified in the notification.

For the SSC MTS & Havaldar Examination 2024, the educational qualification was required as on 01-08-2024.

Candidates possessing equivalent educational qualifications must produce the relevant equivalence certificate from the concerned authorities when required.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India
  • Subject of Nepal
  • Subject of Bhutan
  • Person of Indian origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India
  • Candidates belonging to specified categories requiring an eligibility certificate must obtain the certificate issued by the Government of India.

Age Limit

18 – 25 Years

For MTS posts, the age limit is generally 18–25 years.

For Havaldar in CBIC and CBN and a few MTS posts, the age limit is 18–27 years.

The crucial date for age reckoning in the 2024 notification was 01-08-2024.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum qualification for SSC MTS?

Matriculation / Class 10 pass or equivalent from a recognized Board.

What is the age limit for SSC MTS?

18–25 years for MTS posts. For Havaldar and certain MTS posts, the age limit is 18–27 years.

Is there negative marking in SSC MTS?

There is no negative marking in Session-I. In Session-II, 1 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

Is PET / PST required for MTS?

No. PET / PST is applicable for the Havaldar post.

What is the application fee?

The application fee is ₹100. Women candidates and eligible SC, ST, PwBD and ESM candidates are exempted from payment of the fee.

What is the pay level of MTS and Havaldar?

Both MTS and Havaldar posts are in Pay Level-1 as per the 7th Pay Commission Pay Matrix.
