Posts Offered

SSC CHSL recruits candidates for various clerical and data entry posts in different Ministries and Government Departments.

Lower Division Clerk (LDC)

Performs office work, maintains records, file handling, typing and document management.

Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)

Works in Central Government ministries assisting officers with clerical and administrative duties.

Data Entry Operator (DEO)

Responsible for data entry, report preparation, database maintenance and computer-based office work.

Data Entry Operator Grade-A

Specialized data entry work in selected government departments with higher responsibility.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Must have passed Class 12 or equivalent from a recognized Board.

Nationality

Indian Citizen or other eligible categories as specified by SSC.

Age

18 to 27 Years

Medical Fitness

Candidates should satisfy the medical standards wherever applicable.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed 12th Standard (10+2) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University.

For certain Data Entry Operator posts, candidates should have passed 12th Standard in the Science stream with Mathematics as one of the subjects.

Equivalent qualifications recognized by the Government of India are also accepted.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India
  • Citizen of Nepal
  • Citizen of Bhutan
  • Tibetan Refugee (as per Government Rules)
  • Persons of Indian Origin migrated from specified countries with eligibility certificate.

Age Limit

18 – 27 Years

Age is calculated according to the date mentioned in the official SSC CHSL notification. Candidates belonging to reserved categories are eligible for age relaxation as per Government rules.

Complete Exam Pattern

Tier-I

Computer Based Examination consisting of Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions.

Tier-II

Computer Based Examination including Objective Papers and Skill/Typing Test wherever applicable.
