How to Use PYQs Effectively

Simply reading Previous Year Questions is not enough. To get the maximum benefit from them, follow a structured approach during your preparation.

1 Study the chapter thoroughly before reviewing its PYQs.
2 Observe which concepts and question types appear repeatedly.
3 Attempt the questions yourself before checking any explanation.
4 Maintain short notes for important tricks and repeated concepts.
5 Revise PYQs regularly along with mock tests for better retention.
Pro Tip: Combine chapter-wise study, PYQs and mock tests to build both conceptual clarity and exam confidence.
