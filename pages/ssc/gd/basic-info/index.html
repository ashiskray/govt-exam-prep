Job Profile

The exact duties depend on the force and assignment.

Constable (GD)

  • General duty and operational responsibilities in the allotted CAPF / SSF.
  • Maintaining security and performing duties assigned by the force.
  • Field, security and other operational duties according to the organization.
  • Candidates recommended for appointment are liable to serve anywhere in India.

Rifleman (GD)

  • General duty responsibilities in Assam Rifles.
  • Security and operational duties as assigned by the organization.
  • Field and other duties according to the requirements of Assam Rifles.
  • Service conditions are governed by the rules of the participating organization.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Must have passed Matriculation / 10th Class Examination from a recognized Board / University on or before 01-01-2026.

Nationality

Candidate must be a citizen of India. CAPF and Assam Rifles vacancies are State / UT / area wise, so domicile / PRC requirements apply.

Age

18 to 23 years as on 01-01-2026. Candidates must be born not before 02-01-2003 and not later than 01-01-2008.

Physical Standards

Candidates shortlisted from the CBE must qualify the prescribed PET and PST, followed by medical examination.

PwD Eligibility

Persons with Disabilities (PwD) are not eligible to apply for this Examination.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed the Matriculation or 10th Class Examination from a recognized Board / University on or before the cut-off date 01-01-2026.

Candidates who have not acquired the essential educational qualification by 01-01-2026 are not eligible.

At Document Verification, candidates must produce the relevant original certificate / marksheet or other acceptable documentary proof showing completion of Matriculation by the prescribed cut-off date.

Nationality & Domicile

  • The candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • CAPF and Assam Rifles vacancies are State / UT / area wise, so candidates must provide the domicile / Permanent Residential Certificate (PRC) of the State / UT indicated in the Online Application Form, as applicable.
  • A mismatch between the State / UT declared in the application and the domicile / PRC can lead to cancellation of candidature.
  • Assam candidates are not required to submit a Domicile / PRC because the State of Assam is not issuing one; residential status will be verified by the concerned District Authority.
  • West Pakistani Refugees settled in Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh have the specific documentation provision mentioned in the notification.

PET / PST

Candidates shortlisted from the CBE must qualify the prescribed Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test.

Male PET

5 Kms in 24 minutes

For candidates other than those belonging to the Ladakh Region.

Female PET

1.6 Kms in 8½ minutes

For candidates other than those belonging to the Ladakh Region.

Ladakh Region

Male: 1.6 Kms in 7 minutes

Female: 800 metres in 5 minutes

Basic PST

Male Height: 170 cm

Female Height: 157 cm

Male Chest: 80 cm un-expanded + 5 cm minimum expansion

Female candidates are not measured for chest; the notification states that it will be ascertained that the chest is well-developed. Weight must be proportionate to height and age according to medical standards.

The notification also prescribes category / region-wise relaxations in height and chest standards. Candidates claiming such relaxation must produce the required certificate where applicable.

Medical Examination

Candidates who qualify the PET/PST are considered for the Detailed Medical Examination (DME) / Review Medical Examination (RME) and Document Verification as prescribed.

Medical examination is conducted by Medical Boards constituted by the CAPFs according to the applicable uniform guidelines and Government instructions.

The medical examination assesses the candidate's physical and medical fitness for appointment in the concerned force.

Document Verification includes verification of the Matriculation certificate, domicile / PRC, NCC certificate where applicable, category certificates and other documents required under the notification.

  • One-Time Registration (OTR) must be completed on the new SSC website. OTR generated on the old SSC website is not functional on the new website.
  • The last date for submission of the Online Application Form in the notification is 31-12-2025 at 23:00 hours.
  • The last date for online fee payment is 01-01-2026 at 23:00 hours.
  • The correction window is 08-01-2026 to 10-01-2026 up to 23:00 hours.
  • Candidates must be extremely careful while entering the domicile State / UT and district information.
  • Date of Birth must match the Matriculation / Secondary Examination Certificate.
  • Candidates should check the photograph and signature requirements before final submission.
  • Candidates should give all seven force preferences carefully in the Online Application Form.
  • Admission Certificates for the stages of the examination are issued online; they are not sent by post.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum qualification for SSC GD 2026?

Matriculation / 10th Class Examination passed from a recognized Board / University on or before 01-01-2026.

What is the age limit for SSC GD 2026?

18–23 years as on 01-01-2026. Candidates must be born between 02-01-2003 and 01-01-2008, inclusive.

How many questions are there in SSC GD CBE?

There are 80 objective questions carrying 2 marks each, for a maximum of 160 marks.

Is there negative marking in SSC GD?

Yes. 0.25 mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the Computer Based Examination.

What is the PET running requirement?

For candidates other than those belonging to the Ladakh Region, male candidates have to complete 5 Kms in 24 minutes and female candidates 1.6 Kms in 8½ minutes. Ladakh Region standards are 1.6 Kms in 7 minutes for males and 800 metres in 5 minutes for females.

What is the application fee?

The fee is ₹100. Women candidates and eligible SC, ST and ESM candidates are exempted from payment of the fee.

What is the pay scale?

The pay scale is Pay Level-3: ₹21,700–₹69,100 for all the posts covered by this examination.

How is final selection made?

Final selection and force allocation are based on normalized CBE marks including applicable NCC bonus marks, subject to qualifying PET/PST, medical examination, document verification and other prescribed conditions.
