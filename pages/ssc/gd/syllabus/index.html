Official Syllabus Note

The syllabus on this page is based on the indicative syllabus given in Para 12.1.11 of the official Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026 notification dated 01.12.2025. The notification states that the General Intelligence and Reasoning section is principally non-verbal, General Awareness covers current events and India and neighbouring countries with the subjects specified above, Elementary Mathematics includes the listed arithmetic areas, and English/Hindi tests basic language understanding and basic comprehension.
