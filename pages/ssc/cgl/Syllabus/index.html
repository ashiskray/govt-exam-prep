Tier-II Paper-I

Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)

The Data Entry Speed Test is qualifying in nature and is conducted to assess the candidate's typing speed on a computer.

Purpose of DEST

The test evaluates the ability of candidates to accurately enter data on a computer within the prescribed time limit.

Particular Details
Nature Qualifying
Mode Computer Based
Language English
Time Allowed 15 Minutes
Typing Speed Approximately 8,000 Key Depressions per Hour (KDPH)

Important Points

  • DEST is conducted on a computer.
  • Candidates must type the given passage accurately.
  • Speed as well as accuracy are important.
  • The test is qualifying in nature.
  • DEST is mandatory for eligible posts notified by SSC.
SSC CGL

Syllabus Summary

The SSC Combined Graduate Level Examination evaluates candidates across multiple subjects through Tier-I and Tier-II examinations.

Tier-I

General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Comprehension.

Tier-II

Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning & General Intelligence, English Language & Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Test and Data Entry Speed Test.

Statistics

Applicable only for the Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) post as prescribed in the official notification.

Finance & Economics

Applicable only for the Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) and Assistant Accounts Officer (AAO) posts.

Official Note

Candidates are advised to prepare according to the latest SSC Combined Graduate Level Examination Notification. The Commission reserves the right to modify the examination pattern or syllabus through future notifications.

SSC CGL

Preparation Strategy

A well-planned preparation strategy can significantly improve your chances of qualifying the SSC Combined Graduate Level Examination.

01

Understand the Syllabus

Study every topic mentioned in the official syllabus before starting your preparation. Do not skip any section.

02

Build Strong Fundamentals

Strengthen concepts in Mathematics, English, Reasoning, General Awareness and Computer Knowledge before attempting advanced questions.

03

Practice Previous Year Questions

Solve SSC CGL Previous Year Question Papers regularly to understand the pattern, difficulty level and frequently asked topics.

04

Attempt Mock Tests

Take full-length mock tests regularly and analyse your performance after every test.

05

Improve Speed & Accuracy

Focus on time management, reduce silly mistakes and improve accuracy to maximize your score.

06

Revise Regularly

Make short notes and revise important formulas, grammar rules, current affairs and important facts periodically.

Preparation Tip

Consistency is more important than studying for long hours. Daily practice, proper revision and regular mock tests are the key to success in SSC CGL.
