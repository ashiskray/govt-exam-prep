Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or Institution.

Nationality

Indian Citizen or other eligible categories as specified by SSC.

Age

18–32 Years
(Depends on Post)

Medical Fitness

Candidates must satisfy the Physical, Medical and Eligibility standards wherever applicable.

Educational Qualification

General Posts

Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent on or before the prescribed cut-off date.

Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)

Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th Standard OR Bachelor's Degree with Statistics as one of the subjects at Graduation level.

Statistical Investigator Grade-II

Bachelor's Degree with Statistics as one of the subjects in all three years or all six semesters of Graduation.

Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer

Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University.

Desirable Qualification: CA / CMA / CS / M.Com / MBA (Finance) / Master's in Business Economics.

Nationality

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Person of Indian Origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia or Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • Candidates belonging to the above categories (except Indian citizens) must possess a Certificate of Eligibility issued by the Government of India wherever applicable.

Examination Pattern

SSC CGL examination is conducted in two stages: Tier-I and Tier-II.

Stage - 1

Tier-I Examination

Tier-I is a Computer Based Examination consisting of Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions.

Mode of Exam Computer Based Examination (CBT)
Question Type Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
Total Questions 100
Total Marks 200
Duration 60 Minutes
Language English & Hindi (Except English Section)
Negative Marking 0.50 Mark for each wrong answer.

Tier-I Subject-wise Pattern

Subject Questions Marks
General Intelligence & Reasoning 25 50
General Awareness 25 50
Quantitative Aptitude 25 50
English Comprehension 25 50
Total 100 200

Important Highlights

Online Exam

Tier-I is conducted completely in Computer Based Mode.

100 Questions

Equal weightage is given to all four subjects.

60 Minutes

Candidates get one hour to complete the examination.

Negative Marking

0.50 marks are deducted for every incorrect answer.

Marking Scheme

  • Each correct answer carries 2 marks.
  • Each incorrect answer attracts a penalty of 0.50 marks.
  • No marks are deducted for unanswered questions.
  • The examination is conducted in online mode.
  • The question paper consists entirely of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Tier-I is qualifying for shortlisting candidates to Tier-II.
Stage - 2

Paper-I is compulsory for all candidates shortlisted in Tier-I.

Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)

  • DEST is conducted in Session-II of Paper-I.
  • The test is qualifying in nature.
  • Candidates are required to type at a prescribed speed on a computer.
  • DEST is mandatory for posts where it is applicable as notified by SSC.
  • Failure to qualify DEST may lead to disqualification for applicable posts.

Important Notes

  • Paper-I is compulsory for all candidates.
  • Questions are objective type and computer-based.
  • English questions are available only in English.
  • Except the English section, questions are available in both English and Hindi.
  • Negative marking is applicable as prescribed by SSC for Paper-I sections.
  • Candidates should carefully read the official notification for post-specific requirements.

Important Examination Instructions

  • Carry a valid original photo identity proof to the examination centre.
  • Reach the examination venue well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Electronic gadgets, calculators, smart watches, Bluetooth devices and other prohibited items are not permitted inside the examination hall.
  • Follow all instructions displayed on the computer screen before starting the examination.
  • Use rough sheets only if provided by the examination authority and return them before leaving the examination hall.
  • Maintain discipline throughout the examination. Any unfair means may result in cancellation of candidature.
  • Biometric verification and photograph capture may be carried out during different stages of the recruitment process.
  • Candidates should regularly visit the SSC official website for notices, admit cards, answer keys and result updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an interview in SSC CGL?

No. SSC CGL selection does not include an interview.

Can final year students apply?

Only if they fulfil the educational qualification by the prescribed cut-off date.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. Negative marking is applicable as prescribed for Tier-I and Tier-II.

Can I change my examination centre later?

No. Examination centre changes are generally not permitted after final submission.

Is Aadhaar mandatory?

Candidates should follow the identity proof requirements mentioned in the official notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no fixed attempt limit. Candidates can apply until they exceed the maximum age limit.
