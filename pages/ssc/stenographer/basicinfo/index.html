Posts Offered

The examination is conducted for direct recruitment to Stenographer Grade ‘C’ and Grade ‘D’ posts in Government of India Ministries, Departments and Organizations.

Stenographer Grade ‘C’

Group ‘B’, Non-Gazetted post in various Ministries, Departments, Organizations, Attached offices, Subordinate offices and Statutory bodies of the Government of India.

Stenographer Grade ‘D’

Group ‘C’ post in various Ministries, Departments, Organizations, Attached offices, Subordinate offices and Statutory bodies of the Government of India.

Post Classification

The 2026 examination notice classifies the two posts as follows.

Post Group Status
Stenographer Grade ‘C’ Group ‘B’ Non-Gazetted
Stenographer Grade ‘D’ Group ‘C’ Government Post

Job Profile

The posts are stenography-based positions in various Government of India Ministries, Departments and Organizations.

Stenographer Grade ‘C’

  • Group ‘B’, Non-Gazetted post
  • Posted in various Central Government Ministries / Departments / Organizations
  • Stenography and transcription are core requirements
  • May be allotted to Attached / Subordinate offices and Statutory bodies
  • All India Service Liability applies

Stenographer Grade ‘D’

  • Group ‘C’ post
  • Posted in various Central Government Ministries / Departments / Organizations
  • Stenography and transcription are core requirements
  • May be allotted to Attached / Subordinate offices and Statutory bodies
  • All India Service Liability applies

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification

12th Standard or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University on or before 01.08.2026.

Nationality

Candidates must satisfy the nationality / citizenship requirements prescribed by SSC.

Age

Grade ‘C’: 18–30 years.

Grade ‘D’: 18–27 years.

Stenography Skill

Only candidates who have skills in stenography are eligible to apply.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed 12th standard or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University as on or before the cut-off date, i.e. 01.08.2026.

Candidates must be able to produce the relevant certificate / marksheet / provisional certificate as proof of the required educational qualification during Document Verification when called by the concerned User Department / Organization.

Candidates possessing equivalent educational qualifications may be required to produce the relevant Equivalence Certificate from the concerned authorities.

Nationality / Citizenship

  • Citizen of India
  • Subject of Nepal
  • Subject of Bhutan
  • Person of Indian origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India
  • Candidates belonging to categories requiring an eligibility certificate must obtain the certificate issued by the Government of India.

Syllabus

The Computer Based Examination consists of General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness and English Language & Comprehension.

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, analogies, similarities and differences, space visualization, problem solving, analysis, judgment, decision making, visual memory, classification, series and arithmetical reasoning.

General Awareness

Current events, everyday observation, Sports, History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Polity including Indian Constitution and Scientific Research.

English Language & Comprehension

Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, correct usage and writing ability.

Post Preference & Allocation

  • Candidates have to indicate whether they are applying for Stenographer Grade ‘C’, Grade ‘D’ or both.
  • Post and Department preferences are collected through the Online Option Form before the First Round of Tentative Allocation / result.
  • A candidate will not be considered for a post / Ministry / Department / Organization if the required preference has not been indicated.
  • Once submitted, post preferences are treated as final and cannot be changed subsequently.
  • Candidates are advised to give maximum preferences to improve their possibility of selection.
  • Posts in Border Roads Organization, if vacancies are reported, have stringent physical and medical requirements.

Important Instructions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum qualification for SSC Stenographer?

Candidates must have passed 12th Standard or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University on or before 01.08.2026.

What is the age limit for SSC Stenographer?

For Grade ‘C’, the age limit is 18–30 years. For Grade ‘D’, the age limit is 18–27 years as on 01.08.2026.

Is stenography skill mandatory?

Yes. The notification states that only candidates who have skills in stenography are eligible to apply.

How many marks is the Computer Based Examination?

The Computer Based Examination carries 200 questions and 200 marks: 50 Reasoning, 50 General Awareness and 100 English Language & Comprehension.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. 0.25 marks are deducted for every wrong answer in the Computer Based Examination.

Is the Stenography Skill Test qualifying?

Yes. The Skill Test is mandatory but qualifying in nature. Final selection is based on merit in the Computer Based Examination among candidates who qualify the Skill Test.

What is the application fee?

The application fee is ₹100. Women candidates and eligible SC, ST, PwBD and ESM candidates are exempted from payment of the fee.

What is the tentative vacancy?

The notification mentions approximately 731 tentative vacancies. Updated post-wise and category-wise vacancies will be made available by SSC later.
