Post Details

IBPS Clerk recruitment is conducted for Customer Service Associate positions in participating Public Sector Banks.

Customer Service Associate

The CRP recruits candidates for the Customer Service Associate (CSA) post in participating Public Sector Banks.

Participating Banks

Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, Punjab & Sind Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India.

Pay Scale

The notified basic pay scale starts at ₹24,050 with applicable increments and allowances / perquisites according to the rules of the participating bank.

Career Entry

The recruitment process provides entry into the clerical cadre of participating Public Sector Banks through a common selection process.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements prescribed in the official notification.

Qualification

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government.

Nationality

Eligible candidates include citizens of India and the other categories of persons specified in the official notification, subject to the prescribed certificate of eligibility where required.

Age

Minimum 20 years and maximum 28 years, subject to the applicable upper-age relaxations.

Local Language

Candidates applying for a State / UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified / opted local language.

Educational Qualification

Graduation in Any Discipline

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government is required.

Degree / Marksheet Requirement

The candidate must possess a valid marksheet / degree certificate showing that he or she is a graduate on the day of registration and must indicate the percentage of marks obtained in Graduation in the online application.

Final Result Declaration

The result of the qualifying degree must have been declared by the prescribed date mentioned in the notification. Proper documentary proof from the University / Institution has to be produced during document verification / joining when required.

Percentage / CGPA

Graduation percentage is to be calculated to the nearest two decimals. Where CGPA / OGPA is awarded, it must be converted into percentage according to the conversion criteria of the University / Institution and the required proof must be produced when asked.

Local Language Requirement

Candidates applying for vacancies of a particular State / UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified / opted local language of that State / UT.

Nationality / Citizenship

  • A Citizen of India.
  • A subject of Nepal.
  • A subject of Bhutan.
  • A Tibetan Refugee who came over to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • A person of Indian origin who has migrated from the specified countries mentioned in the notification with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • For categories other than an Indian citizen, the required Certificate of Eligibility issued by the Government of India must be produced wherever applicable.

Age Limit

20 – 28 Years

The prescribed minimum age is 20 years and the maximum age is 28 years, subject to the applicable category-wise upper-age relaxation rules.

The age limit is applicable as prescribed in the official notification. Reserved and other eligible categories receive relaxation as specified by IBPS.

Examination Pattern

The IBPS Clerk / Customer Service Associate recruitment process consists of Online Preliminary Examination, Online Main Examination, Local Language Proficiency Test and Provisional Allotment.

Stage - 1

Online Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is an objective test used to shortlist candidates for the Online Main Examination.

Test Questions Marks Time Medium
English Language 30 30 20 Minutes English
Numerical Ability 35 35 20 Minutes State / UT applicable medium
Reasoning Ability 35 35 20 Minutes State / UT applicable medium
Total 100 100 60 Minutes —

Candidates have to qualify in each of the three tests by securing the cut-off marks decided by IBPS. An adequate number of candidates in each category, as decided by IBPS depending upon requirements, are shortlisted for the Online Main Examination.

Preliminary Examination Subjects

Subject-wise structure of the IBPS Clerk Preliminary Examination.

English Language

30 questions, 30 marks and 20 minutes. The test is conducted in English.

Numerical Ability

35 questions, 35 marks and 20 minutes.

Reasoning Ability

35 questions, 35 marks and 20 minutes.

Stage - 2

Online Main Examination

The Main Examination consists of objective tests in General / Financial Awareness, General English, Reasoning Ability and Quantitative Aptitude. The tests are separately timed.

Test Questions Marks Time Medium
General / Financial Awareness 40 50 20 Minutes Applicable State / UT medium
General English 40 40 35 Minutes English
Reasoning Ability 40 60 35 Minutes Applicable State / UT medium
Quantitative Aptitude 40 50 35 Minutes Applicable State / UT medium
Total 160 200 125 Minutes —

The Main Examination consists of 160 objective questions carrying 200 marks. Each test is separately timed.

Main Examination Subjects

Major sections included in the Online Main Examination.

General / Financial Awareness

40 questions, 50 marks and 20 minutes.

General English

40 questions, 40 marks and 35 minutes.

Reasoning Ability

40 questions, 60 marks and 35 minutes.

Quantitative Aptitude

40 questions, 50 marks and 35 minutes.

Negative Marking

  • Negative marking applies to objective tests in both the Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • If a question is left blank, no penalty is imposed for that question.
  • The corrected score is calculated after applying the prescribed penalty for wrong answers.

Local Language Proficiency Test

Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) is an important part of the selection process for the Customer Service Associate post.

Local Language

Candidates applying for a State / UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified / opted local language.

Qualifying Test

The Local Language Proficiency Test is qualifying in nature.

Language Proof

Candidates who produce the required 10th standard or above marksheet / certificate showing study of the specified / opted local language may not be required to undergo the language test.

Failure in LLPT

Candidates who fail to qualify the required Local Language Proficiency Test will not be offered appointment.

Recruitment Process

Preliminary Exam

Three objective tests are conducted separately within a total duration of 60 minutes.

Main Examination

Four separately timed objective tests are conducted for a total of 160 questions and 200 marks.

Local Language Test

Eligible candidates have to qualify in the specified / opted Local Language Proficiency Test where applicable.

Provisional Allotment

Successful candidates are provisionally allotted to participating banks based on merit, State / UT vacancies and applicable conditions.

Final Merit & Provisional Allotment

  • Marks obtained in the Online Main Examination are considered for final merit listing.
  • Candidates must qualify in the Online Main Examination and secure sufficiently high merit for provisional allotment.
  • The total marks allotted for the Online Main Examination are 200 and these marks are converted out of 100 for provisional allotment.
  • Provisional allotment is made based on State / UT-wise vacancies, merit-cum-preference and applicable reservation / administrative conditions.
  • Candidates can apply for vacancies in any one State / UT only.
  • Final appointment is subject to the applicable eligibility, Local Language Proficiency Test, document verification and other requirements of the participating bank.

How to Apply Online

Application is submitted online through the official IBPS website.

1. Register

Visit the official IBPS website and register for CRP-CSA using the prescribed online application process.

2. Enter Details

Fill personal, educational, category, State / UT and other required details carefully.

3. Upload Documents

Upload the prescribed photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and applicable certificates.

4. Pay Fee

Pay the applicable application fee / intimation charges through the online payment facility.

5. Submit

Submit the completed application and retain the system-generated application form, Registration Number and Password.

Documents / Upload Requirements

Important items required during online registration and subsequent stages.

Photograph

A recent recognisable photograph of the prescribed size must be uploaded. Candidates should retain copies for later stages.

Signature

Signature should be clear and appropriately sized. Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is not accepted.

Left Thumb Impression

The prescribed left thumb impression must be scanned and uploaded clearly without smudging or blurring.

Handwritten Declaration

The declaration must be written by the candidate in the prescribed manner and uploaded as required.

Graduation Documents

Valid marksheet / degree certificate and proof of percentage / CGPA conversion where applicable.

Category Certificates

SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS / PwBD / ESM and other applicable certificates must be produced in the prescribed format when required.

Important Application Instructions

  • Only one application should be submitted by a candidate. In case of multiple applications, only the latest valid completed application is retained and fees paid for other registrations are forfeited.
  • Online applications once registered cannot be withdrawn and the fee once paid is not refundable.
  • Candidates should carefully fill personal, educational, category, State / UT and other details in the application.
  • The State / UT option for provisional allotment, once exercised, is irrevocable.
  • The uploaded photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and applicable certificate must be clear and properly uploaded.
  • The candidate must retain the Registration Number, Password and system-generated application form for future reference.
  • Incorrect or incomplete information can lead to rejection or disqualification at any stage.

Examination Centre & Call Letter Instructions

  • The examinations are conducted online at designated centres.
  • Candidates must carry the valid call letter and original photo identity proof as required.
  • Candidates called for the Main Examination must bring the authenticated / stamped Preliminary Examination call letter and authenticated / stamped copy of the ID proof as prescribed.
  • Candidates reporting after the reporting time specified in the call letter are not permitted to take the examination.
  • Rough sheets provided at the candidate's desk must be submitted after the examination as instructed at the venue.

Pre-Examination Training

Training may be arranged for eligible candidates who opt for it.

Eligible Categories

Eligible candidates belonging to specified categories may be provided Pre-Examination Training as prescribed by IBPS.

Mode

Pre-Examination Training may be arranged in online mode.

Opt During Application

Eligible candidates who wish to avail the facility should indicate it in the relevant column of the online application.

No Selection Right

Attending Pre-Examination Training does not give a candidate any right to selection or appointment.

PwBD / Scribe Provisions

Special provisions are available to eligible candidates as prescribed by the notification.

Scribe Facility

Eligible candidates may use a scribe subject to the conditions, declarations and documentation prescribed by IBPS.

Compensatory Time

Eligible candidates are entitled to compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour or otherwise advised, subject to the applicable provisions.

Visually Impaired Candidates

Eligible visually impaired candidates may opt for magnified font and are entitled to the applicable compensatory-time provision.

Certificates

Disability certificates and other prescribed documents must be produced / uploaded wherever required.

Important Points to Remember

  • IBPS conducts the Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associates in participating Public Sector Banks.
  • Qualification is graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or an equivalent recognised qualification.
  • Candidates applying for a State / UT should possess proficiency in the specified / opted local language.
  • The Preliminary Examination is used for shortlisting candidates for the Main Examination.
  • The Main Examination consists of 160 questions for 200 marks and is separately timed by section.
  • Only Main Examination marks are considered for final merit listing.
  • The Local Language Proficiency Test is qualifying in nature and is conducted before joining where applicable.
  • Candidates who fail the required Local Language Proficiency Test will not be offered appointment.
  • Provisional allotment depends on State / UT-wise vacancies, merit-cum-preference and applicable reservation / administrative conditions.
  • All candidates should regularly check the official IBPS website for updates, call letters and further instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IBPS Clerk?

IBPS Clerk is commonly used for the recruitment conducted through CRP-CSA for the Customer Service Associate (CSA) post in participating Public Sector Banks.

What is the educational qualification for IBPS Clerk?

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government is required.

What is the age limit for IBPS Clerk?

The prescribed age is 20 to 28 years, subject to applicable upper-age relaxations for eligible categories.

What is the IBPS Clerk application fee?

₹175 inclusive of GST for SC / ST / PwBD / ESM / DESM candidates and ₹850 inclusive of GST for all other candidates. Online payment transaction charges are borne by the candidate.

How many questions are in the Preliminary Examination?

The Preliminary Examination has 100 questions for 100 marks: 30 English Language, 35 Numerical Ability and 35 Reasoning Ability questions.

What is the IBPS Clerk Main Examination pattern?

The Main Examination consists of 160 objective questions carrying 200 marks with a total time of 125 minutes. The four tests are separately timed.

Is there negative marking in IBPS Clerk?

Yes. One-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for every wrong answer in the objective tests of the Preliminary and Main Examination.

Is there a Local Language Proficiency Test?

Yes. Candidates applying for a State / UT are required to be proficient in the specified / opted local language. The Local Language Proficiency Test is qualifying in nature where applicable.

What happens if I fail the Local Language Proficiency Test?

Candidates who fail to qualify the required Local Language Proficiency Test will not be offered appointment.

What is the starting basic pay of IBPS Clerk?

The starting basic pay for Customer Service Associate is ₹24,050, with applicable increments, allowances and perquisites according to the rules of the participating bank.

Which banks participate in IBPS Clerk?

The notification lists Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, Punjab & Sind Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India.

Can I apply for more than one State / UT?

No. Since recruitment in the clerical cadre is conducted on State / UT-wise basis, a candidate can apply for vacancies in any one State / UT only.

Can I submit more than one application?

No. More than one application should not be submitted. If multiple applications are submitted, only the latest valid completed application is retained and fees paid for other registrations are forfeited.
