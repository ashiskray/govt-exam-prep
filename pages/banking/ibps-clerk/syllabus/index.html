Preliminary Examination — Official Conditions

  • Candidates have to qualify in each of the three Preliminary tests by securing the cut-off marks decided by IBPS.
  • An adequate number of candidates in each category, as decided by IBPS depending upon requirements, will be shortlisted for the Online Main Examination.
  • English Language, Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability are separately timed.
  • Each Preliminary test is allotted 20 minutes.
  • The total duration of the Preliminary Examination is 60 minutes.

Main Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Main Examination consists of four objective tests.
  • The four Main Examination tests are separately timed.
  • The Main Examination contains 160 questions for a maximum of 200 marks.
  • The total duration of the Main Examination is 125 minutes.
  • Candidates are required to obtain a minimum score in each test and also a minimum total score to be considered for further process.
  • Marks obtained in the Online Main Examination only are considered for final merit listing.

Negative Marking

  • There is a penalty for wrong answers in the objective tests of both the Online Preliminary and Online Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • If a question is left blank and no answer is marked, there is no penalty for that question.
  • The penalty is applied to arrive at the corrected score.

Scoring & Normalization

Corrected Score

Scores are corrected after applying the prescribed penalty for wrong answers.

Normalization

Where applicable, corrected scores obtained in different sessions are normalized using the equi-percentile method.

Two Decimal Places

Scores up to two decimal points are taken for calculation purposes.

Final Merit

Only marks obtained in the Online Main Examination are considered for final merit listing.

Local Language Proficiency Test

The Local Language Proficiency Test is part of the selection process before joining the participating bank.

Local Language

Candidates applying for a particular State / UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified / opted local language.

One Language Option

Candidates have to opt for one language for the Local Language Test from the list provided for the relevant State / UT.

10th Language Certificate

Candidates who produce a 10th standard or above marksheet / certificate showing that they studied the specified / opted local language are not required to undergo the language test.

Qualifying Nature

The Local Language Proficiency Test is marks based and qualifying in nature.

Failure in LLPT

Candidates who fail to qualify the required Local Language Proficiency Test will not be offered appointment and will be disqualified from the process.

Before Joining

The Local Language Proficiency Test is conducted after qualifying the Online Main Examination / Provisional Allotment but before joining the Bank.

IBPS Clerk Selection Process

The official CRP-CSA-XVI recruitment process consists of the Online Preliminary Examination, Online Main Examination, Local Language Proficiency Test and provisional allotment.

Stage 1 — Preliminary

Candidates appear for the Online Preliminary Examination. Candidates who qualify and are shortlisted proceed to the Main Examination.

Stage 2 — Main

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Online Main Examination consisting of four objective tests.

Stage 3 — LLPT

Candidates are required to qualify the applicable Local Language Proficiency Test before joining, unless exempted through accepted language-study proof.

Stage 4 — Provisional Allotment

Candidates sufficiently high in merit are provisionally allotted to participating banks based on merit-cum-preference and actual vacancies.
