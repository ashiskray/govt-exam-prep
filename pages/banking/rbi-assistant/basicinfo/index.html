Gross Emoluments & Perquisites

At present, initial monthly gross emoluments without HRA are approximately ₹58,514. HRA of 15% of Pay is additionally paid if the employee is not staying in Bank accommodation. Other benefits include accommodation subject to availability, medical facilities, furnishing allowance, festival advance, leave fare concession and eligible loans/advances.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements prescribed in the official RBI notification.

Qualification

Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate; pass class for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

Nationality

Citizen of India or other eligible categories specified in the notification, subject to Certificate of Eligibility where required.

Age

20–28 years as on 01/02/2026, subject to applicable relaxation.

Language

Candidates must know how to read, write, speak and understand the language of the State/States falling under the Recruitment Office.

Educational Qualification

Bachelor’s Degree

At least a bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate and knowledge of word processing on PC. For SC/ST/PwBD candidates, pass class is prescribed.

Ex-Servicemen

An eligible Ex-Serviceman (other than dependents) should either be a graduate from a recognised University or have passed matriculation/equivalent examination of the Armed Forces and rendered at least 15 years of defence service.

Percentage / CGPA

Percentage is calculated by aggregating marks obtained in all subjects and maximum marks in all subjects. A fraction is ignored; therefore 49.99% is treated as below 50%. Where the institution prescribes a CGPA-to-percentage conversion, that method is accepted; otherwise the notification provides prescribed examples for a 10-point scale.

Language Requirement

Candidates applying for a particular Recruitment Office must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the language of the State or any State falling under that office.

Nationality / Citizenship

  • A citizen of India.
  • A subject of Nepal.
  • A subject of Bhutan.
  • A Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • A person of Indian origin who migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, specified East African countries, Ethiopia or Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • For categories other than an Indian citizen, a Certificate of Eligibility from the Government of India is required wherever prescribed.

Age Limit

20 – 28 Years

The age is counted as on 01/02/2026. Candidates born not earlier than 02/02/1998 and not later than 01/02/2006, both dates inclusive, are eligible, subject to the notification’s conditions.

Examination Pattern

Selection consists of Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and Language Proficiency Test (LPT).

Stage 1

Preliminary Examination: 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes.

Stage 2

Main Examination: 200 questions, 200 marks, 135 minutes.

Stage 3

Language Proficiency Test in the official/local language of the concerned State.

Negative Marking

1/4 mark is deducted for each wrong answer in objective tests.

Negative Marking

  • Negative marking applies in the objective tests of both Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, 1/4 of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • A candidate has to qualify in each part of the objective test separately.
  • Approximately 10 times the vacancies may be shortlisted from Prelims and approximately 2 times the vacancies from Main, subject to availability and RBI’s decision.

Language Proficiency Test

LPT is mandatory for provisionally shortlisted candidates from the Main Examination.

Local Language

Conducted in the official/local language(s) of the concerned State.

Qualifying Test

Candidates not proficient in the required language are disqualified.

Regional Office

LPT is conducted at the respective Regional Office of RBI.

No Exemption

The notification states that LPT is mandatory and no exemption is given.

Recruitment Process

Preliminary Exam

Three objective tests are conducted in 60 minutes.

Main Examination

Five separately timed objective tests total 200 questions and 200 marks.

Language Proficiency Test

Candidates shortlisted from Main must qualify in the concerned local language.

Final Selection

Based on online examination merit, qualifying LPT, medical fitness and verification of certificates, etc.

Final Merit & Selection

  • Only candidates sufficiently high in merit in the aggregate marks of the Main Examination are called for LPT.
  • LPT is mandatory and qualifying.
  • Final selection is based on performance in the online examination in order of merit, qualifying LPT, medical fitness and verification of certificates, etc., to RBI’s satisfaction.
  • Appointment is subject to the candidate being declared medically fit under RBI rules.

Important Application Instructions

  • Apply online only; no manual/paper application is entertained.
  • Information furnished in the online application must be accurate and complete.
  • Multiple registrations: the last registration is retained.
  • Documents relating to age, qualification and category are produced at LPT/document verification.
  • Applicants working in Government/PSU/banks and similar organisations must inform their employer before submitting the online application and provide the required undertaking.
  • Late reporting at the examination venue is not permitted.
  • Mobile phones, communication devices and calculators are banned at the examination premises.

Examination Centre & Call Letter Instructions

  • Preliminary and Main Examinations are conducted online at centres across India.
  • Candidates have to select the centre corresponding to the office for which they apply, though RBI may allot another centre as permitted by the notification.
  • No request for change of examination centre is entertained.
  • Call letters for Preliminary and Main Examination are downloaded from the RBI website; intimation is also sent by email/SMS.
  • Candidates must bring the call letter, stipulated photo ID proof in original and a photocopy of the same ID.
  • Candidates appear at their own cost and risk.

Pre-Examination Training

Training may be arranged for eligible SC/ST/OBC/PwBD candidates.

Eligible Categories

SC/ST/OBC/PwBD candidates may request pre-examination training subject to the Bank’s arrangements.

How to Request

The request is sent to the concerned RBI Regional Office using the prescribed format.

Medium

Training may be in English/Hindi; Hindi training is subject to sufficient registrations.

Expenses

Travelling, boarding and lodging expenses for the training are borne by the candidate.

PwBD / Scribe Provisions

Benchmark Disability

PwBD reservation and facilities are governed by the RPwD Act, 2016 and Government instructions.

Visually Impaired Candidates

Magnified font is available; candidates with not less than 40% disability are eligible for compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour.

Scribe Facility

Eligible candidates with writing limitations may use a scribe subject to the prescribed conditions and certificates.

Compensatory Time

Eligible PwBD candidates with physical limitation to write/type receive 20 minutes per hour of the examination, whether using a scribe or not.

Important Points to Remember

  • The notification is for 650 RBI Assistant vacancies for Panel Year 2025.
  • Graduation in any discipline with minimum 50% marks is required; SC/ST/PwBD candidates need pass class.
  • Age is 20–28 years as on 01/02/2026, with prescribed relaxations.
  • Selection is through Prelims, Main Examination and mandatory LPT.
  • Prelims has 100 questions/100 marks/60 minutes; Main has 200 questions/200 marks/135 minutes.
  • 1/4 mark is deducted for each wrong answer in objective tests.
  • Initial basic pay is ₹29,000 and initial gross emoluments without HRA are approximately ₹58,514.
  • LPT failure results in disqualification.
  • All claims regarding category, age relaxation and disability benefits require prescribed certificates.
  • RBI may change examination dates, centres or vacancies as permitted by the notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RBI Assistant?

RBI Assistant is a clerical-level post in the Reserve Bank of India recruited through a country-wide competitive examination followed by a Language Proficiency Test.

What is the educational qualification?

At least a bachelor’s degree in any discipline with 50% marks in aggregate; pass class is prescribed for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, along with knowledge of word processing on PC.

What is the age limit?

20 to 28 years as on 01/02/2026. The notification provides category-wise upper-age relaxation.

How many vacancies are notified?

650 Assistant vacancies are notified for Panel Year 2025.

What is the Preliminary Examination pattern?

English Language 30, Numerical Ability 35 and Reasoning Ability 35 questions: 100 questions, 100 marks and 60 minutes.

What is the Main Examination pattern?

Reasoning, English Language, Numerical Ability, General Awareness and Computer Knowledge: 200 questions, 200 marks and 135 minutes.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. One-fourth of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in the objective tests.

Is LPT compulsory?

Yes. Candidates shortlisted from Main must undergo the Language Proficiency Test and candidates not proficient in the required local language are disqualified.

What is the starting basic pay?

₹29,000 per month. The notification states initial monthly gross emoluments without HRA are approximately ₹58,514.

What is the application fee?

SC/ST/PwBD/EXS candidates pay ₹50 plus 18% GST as intimation charges; GEN/OBC/EWS candidates pay ₹450 plus 18% GST including intimation charges.

Can I apply offline?

No. The notification permits online application only through the RBI website.

Which local language is required for Kolkata office?

Bengali / Nepali, as listed in Appendix-I of the notification.
