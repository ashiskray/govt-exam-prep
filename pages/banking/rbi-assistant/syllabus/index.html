Important Syllabus & Exam Rules

  • Candidates have to qualify in each part of the Objective test separately.
  • Candidates must qualify in the Preliminary Examination to appear in the Main Examination.
  • There is negative marking in both Preliminary and Main Objective Tests.
  • One-fourth (1/4th) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer.
  • Candidates are shortlisted from Preliminary Examination approximately 10 times the number of vacancies, subject to availability.
  • Candidates are shortlisted from Main Examination approximately 2 times the number of vacancies, subject to availability.
  • The Language Proficiency Test is mandatory for candidates shortlisted from the Main Examination.
  • A candidate not proficient in the applicable Official / Local Language shall be disqualified.

Medium of Examination

The notification specifies different examination mediums for different RBI Recruitment Offices.

English Test

The Test of English Language is conducted in English.

Other Objective Tests

Other online tests are available in English, Hindi and the additional languages specified for the respective Recruitment Office.

Kolkata Office

English, Hindi & Bengali are available as the medium of online examination.

Language Proficiency Test (LPT)

The LPT is the final language-based stage after the Main Examination.

Local Language

The LPT is conducted in the Official / Local Language(s) of the concerned state.

Mandatory

No exemption is given from appearing in the LPT.

Disqualification

Candidates not proficient in the applicable local language shall be disqualified.

Kolkata LPT

Bengali / Nepali are the local languages specified for the Kolkata Recruitment Office.

Official Syllabus Scope — Important Note

  • The RBI Assistant Panel Year 2025 notification officially specifies the names of the tests conducted in the Preliminary and Main Examinations.
  • The Preliminary Examination officially consists of English Language, Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability.
  • The Main Examination officially consists of Test of Reasoning, Test of English Language, Test of Numerical Ability, Test of General Awareness and Test of Computer Knowledge.
  • The notification does not provide a detailed chapter-wise list of topics under these tests.
  • Therefore, no coaching-source or unofficial chapter list has been presented as an official RBI syllabus on this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official RBI Assistant Preliminary syllabus?

The official notification specifies three Preliminary tests: English Language, Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability.

How many questions are in RBI Assistant Prelims?

The Preliminary Examination contains 100 questions for 100 marks: 30 English Language, 35 Numerical Ability and 35 Reasoning Ability.

What is the official RBI Assistant Main syllabus?

The Main Examination consists of Test of Reasoning, Test of English Language, Test of Numerical Ability, Test of General Awareness and Test of Computer Knowledge.

How many questions are in RBI Assistant Mains?

The Main Examination contains 200 questions for 200 marks with a total duration of 135 minutes.

Is there negative marking in RBI Assistant?

Yes. One-fourth (1/4th) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in the Preliminary and Main Objective Tests.

Is the Language Proficiency Test mandatory?

Yes. The LPT is mandatory for candidates provisionally shortlisted from the Main Examination. Candidates not proficient in the applicable Official / Local Language shall be disqualified.

Does RBI provide an official chapter-wise syllabus?

The Panel Year 2025 recruitment notification specifies the examination tests, their questions, marks and timings, but does not provide a chapter-wise topic list. Therefore, this page does not present unofficial topic lists as official RBI syllabus.
