Preliminary Examination — Official Conditions

  • Candidates have to qualify in each of the three Preliminary tests by securing the minimum cut-off marks decided by IBPS.
  • An adequate number of candidates in each category, as decided by IBPS depending upon requirements, will be shortlisted for the Online Main Examination.
  • The three Preliminary tests are separately timed: 20 minutes for English Language, 20 minutes for Quantitative Aptitude and 20 minutes for Reasoning Ability.
  • The total Preliminary Examination duration is 60 minutes.

Main Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Main Examination consists of Objective and Descriptive components.
  • The four objective tests are separately timed.
  • The objective Main Examination has 170 questions for 200 marks and 160 minutes.
  • The Descriptive Paper contains 2 questions for 25 marks with a duration of 30 minutes.
  • The Descriptive Paper is in English and consists of Essay and Comprehension.
  • The notification states that the Descriptive Paper may be broadly based on Economic and Social issues, emerging trends in Banking and Technology, Current events, Ethics etc.

Negative Marking

  • There is a penalty for wrong answers in the objective tests of the CRP online examinations.
  • For every question for which a wrong answer has been given by the candidate, one-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to that question is deducted as a penalty.
  • If a question is left blank, there is no penalty for that question.

Selection Process

The official notification describes the recruitment sequence from Preliminary Examination to provisional allotment.

Stage 1 — Preliminary

Candidates appear in the Online Preliminary Examination. Candidates who qualify and are shortlisted proceed to the Main Examination.

Stage 2 — Main

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Online Main Examination consisting of Objective Tests and the Descriptive Paper.

Stage 3 — Personality Test

Candidates who qualify in the Main Examination are called for the Personality Test. It is non-qualifying but mandatory for Interview eligibility.

Stage 4 — Interview

Shortlisted candidates subsequently appear for the Common Interview conducted by participating banks and coordinated by the Nodal Bank.

Stage 5 — Provisional Allotment

Candidates sufficiently high in merit and qualifying the required stages are considered for provisional allotment to participating banks based on actual vacancies.
