Post Details

IBPS PO / MT recruitment is conducted for Probationary Officer / Management Trainee positions in participating Public Sector Banks.

Probationary Officer

The CRP recruits candidates for the Probationary Officer / Management Trainee post in participating Public Sector Banks.

Participating Banks

Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, Punjab & Sind Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India.

Pay Scale

The notified basic pay scale starts at ₹48,480 with applicable increments and allowances / perquisites according to the rules of the participating bank.

Career Entry

The recruitment process provides entry into the officer cadre of participating Public Sector Banks through a common selection process.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements prescribed in the official notification.

Qualification

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government.

Nationality

Eligible candidates include citizens of India and the other categories of persons specified in the official notification, subject to the prescribed certificate of eligibility where required.

Age

Minimum 20 years and maximum 30 years, subject to the applicable upper-age relaxations.

Final Result

The final graduation result must have been declared by the prescribed cut-off date and the candidate must possess the required marksheet / degree proof.

Educational Qualification

Graduation in Any Discipline

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government is required.

Degree / Marksheet Requirement

The candidate must possess a valid marksheet / degree certificate showing that he or she is a graduate on the day of registration and must indicate the percentage of marks obtained in Graduation in the online application.

Final Result Declaration

The final result of the qualifying degree must have been declared by the date prescribed in the notification. Proper documentary proof from the University / Institution has to be produced at the interview when required.

Percentage / CGPA

Graduation percentage is to be calculated to the nearest two decimals. Where CGPA / OGPA is awarded, it must be converted into percentage according to the conversion criteria of the University / Institution and the required proof must be produced when asked.

Important Note

Candidates awaiting final results of the qualifying graduation examination on the prescribed eligibility date are not eligible under the notification.

Nationality / Citizenship

  • A Citizen of India.
  • A subject of Nepal.
  • A subject of Bhutan.
  • A Tibetan Refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • A person of Indian origin who has migrated from the specified countries mentioned in the notification with the intention of permanently settling in India.
  • For categories other than an Indian citizen, the required Certificate of Eligibility issued by the Government of India must be produced wherever applicable.

Age Limit

20 – 30 Years

The prescribed minimum age is 20 years and the maximum age is 30 years, subject to the applicable category-wise upper-age relaxation rules.

For the notification's stated age reckoning, the candidate must satisfy the prescribed date-of-birth conditions. Reserved and other eligible categories receive relaxation as specified by IBPS.

Age Relaxation

Upper-age relaxation available to eligible categories.

Age relaxation is subject to the detailed conditions of the official notification. Candidates claiming relaxation must produce the prescribed certificates at the interview / subsequent recruitment stage.

Examination Pattern

The IBPS PO / MT recruitment process consists of Online Preliminary Examination, Online Main Examination, Personality Test, Common Interview and Provisional Allotment.

Stage - 1

Online Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is an objective test used to shortlist candidates for the Online Main Examination.

Test Questions Marks Time Medium
English Language 30 30 20 Minutes English
Quantitative Aptitude 35 30 20 Minutes English & Hindi
Reasoning Ability 35 40 20 Minutes English & Hindi
Total 100 100 60 Minutes —

Candidates have to qualify in each of the three tests by securing the minimum cut-off marks decided by IBPS. An adequate number of candidates in each category are shortlisted for the Online Main Examination according to IBPS requirements.

Preliminary Examination Subjects

Subject-wise structure of the IBPS PO Preliminary Examination.

English Language

30 questions, 30 marks and 20 minutes. The test is conducted in English.

Quantitative Aptitude

35 questions, 30 marks and 20 minutes. The test is available in English and Hindi.

Reasoning Ability

35 questions, 40 marks and 20 minutes. The test is available in English and Hindi.

Stage - 2

Online Main Examination

The Main Examination contains objective tests followed by a descriptive paper. The objective tests are separately timed.

Test Questions Marks Time Medium
Reasoning 40 60 45 Minutes English & Hindi
General / Economy / Banking Awareness / Digital / Financial Awareness including RBI circulars 50 60 35 Minutes English & Hindi
English Language 40 20 35 Minutes English
Data Analysis & Interpretation 40 60 45 Minutes English & Hindi
Objective Total 170 200 160 Minutes —
Descriptive Paper — Essay & Comprehension 2 25 30 Minutes English

The Main Examination therefore has an objective component of 170 questions for 200 marks and a descriptive paper of 25 marks. The notification states that the descriptive paper may broadly cover economic and social issues, emerging trends in banking and technology, current events, ethics and related areas.

Main Examination Subjects

Major sections included in the Online Main Examination.

Reasoning

40 questions, 60 marks and 45 minutes.

General / Economy / Banking Awareness

50 questions, 60 marks and 35 minutes, including Digital / Financial Awareness and RBI circulars.

English Language

40 questions, 20 marks and 35 minutes.

Data Analysis & Interpretation

40 questions, 60 marks and 45 minutes.

Descriptive Paper

Essay and Comprehension: 2 questions, 25 marks and 30 minutes in English.

Negative Marking

  • Negative marking applies to objective tests in both the Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • If a question is left blank, no penalty is imposed for that question.
  • The corrected scores may be normalised using the equi-percentile method when required.

Personality Test

The Personality Test is conducted after qualification in the Main Examination.

Self-Report Test

The Personality Test is a self-report assessment and the candidate's personality profile is placed before the Interview panel.

Non-Qualifying

The Personality Test is non-qualifying in nature.

Mandatory

Appearing in the Personality Test is mandatory for being eligible for the Interview process.

Mode

It may be conducted through proctored mode or at identified examination centres as decided by IBPS.

Common Interview

Shortlisted candidates after the Main Examination and Personality Test are called for Interview.

Interview Marks 100
Minimum Qualifying Marks 40% for General candidates; 35% for SC / ST / OBC / PwBD candidates
Main : Interview Weightage 80 : 20
Conducting Arrangement Participating Banks, coordinated by the Nodal Bank in each State / UT / Region with the help of IBPS
Final Score Based on Online Main Examination and Interview scores according to the prescribed 80:20 weightage

Recruitment Process

Preliminary Exam

Three objective tests are conducted separately within a total duration of 60 minutes.

Main Examination

Objective tests followed by a descriptive English paper.

Personality Test

Non-qualifying but mandatory for candidates proceeding to the Interview stage.

Common Interview

Interview carries 100 marks and is conducted by participating banks with IBPS coordination.

Provisional Allotment

Successful candidates are provisionally allotted to participating banks according to vacancies and applicable conditions.

Final Merit & Provisional Allotment

  • Marks obtained in the Online Main Examination are considered for shortlisting for the Personality Test / Interview and for final merit listing.
  • A candidate must qualify in both the Online Main Examination and Interview and must be sufficiently high in merit for subsequent provisional allotment.
  • The relative weightage of Online Main Examination and Interview is 80:20.
  • The Personality Test is mandatory for Interview eligibility but is non-qualifying in nature.
  • Provisional allotment is made to one of the participating banks based on the actual vacancies reported by the banks and applicable reservation / administrative conditions.
  • Final appointment is subject to the applicable eligibility, document verification and other requirements of the participating bank.

How to Apply Online

Application is submitted online through the official IBPS website.

1. Register

Visit the official IBPS website and register for CRP-PO/MT using the prescribed online application process.

2. Enter Details

Fill personal, educational, category and other required details carefully. Certain fields cannot be changed after submission.

3. Upload Documents

Upload the prescribed photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and applicable certificate.

4. Pay Fee

Pay the applicable application fee / intimation charges through the online payment facility.

5. Submit

Submit the completed application and retain the system-generated application form, Registration Number and Password.

Documents / Upload Requirements

Important items required during online registration and subsequent stages.

Photograph

A recent, recognisable photograph of the prescribed size must be uploaded. Candidates must retain copies for later stages.

Signature

Signature should be clear, appropriately sized and consistent throughout the recruitment process. Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is not accepted.

Left Thumb Impression

The prescribed left thumb impression must be scanned and uploaded clearly without smudging or blurring.

Handwritten Declaration

The declaration must be in the candidate's own handwriting and in English, and should not be written in CAPITAL LETTERS.

Graduation Documents

Valid marksheet / degree certificate and proof of percentage / CGPA conversion where applicable.

Category Certificates

SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS / PwBD and other applicable certificates must be produced in the prescribed format when required.

Important Application Instructions

  • Only one application should be submitted by a candidate. In case of multiple applications, only the latest valid completed application is retained and fees paid for other registrations are forfeited.
  • Online applications once registered cannot be withdrawn and the fee once paid is not refundable.
  • Candidate's Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, State / UT, Post and Nationality fields are treated as final after submission and cannot be changed as prescribed in the notification.
  • The mobile number and email ID used in the application should remain active throughout the recruitment process.
  • The uploaded photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and applicable certificate must be clear and not smudged or blurred.
  • The candidate must retain the Registration Number, Password and system-generated application form for future reference.
  • Incorrect or incomplete information can lead to rejection or disqualification at any stage.

Examination Centre & Call Letter Instructions

  • The examinations are conducted online at centres across India.
  • The examination centre allotted by IBPS cannot ordinarily be changed on request.
  • Call letters are downloaded from the official IBPS website; hard copies are not sent by post or courier.
  • Candidates must carry the valid call letter and original photo identity proof as required.
  • For Main Examination, candidates called for the Main stage must also bring the authenticated / stamped Preliminary Examination call letter and authenticated / stamped copy of the ID proof as prescribed.
  • Candidates reporting after the reporting time specified in the call letter are not permitted to take the examination.

Pre-Examination Training

Training may be arranged for eligible candidates who opt for it.

Eligible Categories

Limited candidates belonging to SC, ST, OBC and Minority Communities may be provided Pre-Examination Training.

Mode

Pre-Examination Training may be arranged in online mode.

Opt During Application

Eligible candidates who wish to avail the facility should indicate it in the relevant column of the online application.

No Selection Right

Attending Pre-Examination Training does not give a candidate any right to selection or appointment.

PwBD / Scribe Provisions

Special provisions are available to eligible candidates as prescribed by the notification.

Scribe Facility

Eligible candidates may use a scribe subject to the conditions, declarations and documentation prescribed by IBPS.

Compensatory Time

Eligible candidates using a scribe and specified categories are entitled to compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour or otherwise advised.

Visually Impaired Candidates

Eligible VI candidates may opt for magnified font and are entitled to the applicable compensatory-time provision.

Certificates

Disability certificates and other prescribed documents must be produced / uploaded wherever required.

Important Points to Remember

  • IBPS conducts the Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers / Management Trainees once a year for participating Public Sector Banks.
  • Qualification is graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or an equivalent recognised qualification.
  • Preliminary Examination is only for shortlisting to the Main Examination; candidates must qualify each prescribed test.
  • Only Main Examination marks are considered for shortlisting for Personality Test / Interview and for final merit listing.
  • The Personality Test is mandatory but non-qualifying.
  • Interview carries 100 marks, with minimum qualifying marks of 40% and 35% for SC / ST / OBC / PwBD candidates.
  • Final weightage of Main Examination and Interview is 80:20.
  • Provisional allotment depends on actual vacancies reported by participating banks and applicable reservation / administrative conditions.
  • All candidates should regularly check the official IBPS website for updates, call letters and further instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IBPS PO?

IBPS PO refers to the Common Recruitment Process for recruitment of Probationary Officers / Management Trainees in participating Public Sector Banks.

What is the educational qualification for IBPS PO?

A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government is required.

What is the age limit for IBPS PO?

The prescribed age is 20 to 30 years, subject to applicable upper-age relaxations for eligible categories.

What is the IBPS PO application fee?

₹175 inclusive of GST for SC / ST / PwBD candidates and ₹850 inclusive of GST for all other candidates. Online payment transaction charges are borne by the candidate.

How many questions are in the Preliminary Examination?

The Preliminary Examination has 100 questions for 100 marks: 30 English, 35 Quantitative Aptitude and 35 Reasoning Ability questions.

What is the IBPS PO Main Examination pattern?

The objective Main Examination has 170 questions for 200 marks in 160 minutes, followed by a 25-mark descriptive paper of 30 minutes containing Essay and Comprehension.

Is there negative marking in IBPS PO?

Yes. One-fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for every wrong answer in the objective tests of the Preliminary and Main Examination.

Is the Personality Test qualifying?

No. The Personality Test is non-qualifying, but appearing in it is mandatory for being eligible for the Interview process.

How many marks is the Interview?

The Interview carries 100 marks. The minimum qualifying marks are 40%, with 35% for SC / ST / OBC / PwBD candidates.

What is the final weightage of IBPS PO?

The relative weightage of the Online Main Examination and Interview is 80:20.

Which banks participate in IBPS PO?

The notification lists Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, Punjab & Sind Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India.

Can I apply more than once?

No. More than one application should not be submitted. If multiple applications are submitted, only the latest valid completed application is retained and fees paid for the other registrations are forfeited.
