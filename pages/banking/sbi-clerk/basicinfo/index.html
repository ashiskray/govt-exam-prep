Post Details

SBI recruits candidates for the Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) position in the clerical cadre.

Junior Associate

The post is Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) in the clerical cadre of State Bank of India.

Posting

Candidates are considered against vacancies of the State/UT for which they apply and, if selected, are posted in that State/UT.

Job Profile

The new recruits must have flair for marketing and may be required to make customer calls, provide banking and advisory services and cross sell products.

Transfer Rule

There is no provision for Inter Circle Transfer / Inter State Transfer before completion of 5 years of service, subject to the exceptional conditions specified by SBI.

The total starting emoluments of a Clerical Cadre employee payable at a Metro such as Mumbai will be around ₹46,000 per month, inclusive of DA, other allowances at the current rate and two additional increments for newly recruited graduate Junior Associates. Other Benefits

Eligible employees may receive reimbursement of various perquisites, Provident Fund, Pension under the New Pension Scheme (Defined Contribution Benefit), Medical, Leave-Fare and other facilities as per Bank instructions.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements prescribed in the official notification.

Qualification

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government.

Nationality

Applications are invited from eligible Indian Citizens for appointment as Junior Associate in SBI.

Minimum 20 years and maximum 28 years as on 01.04.2026, subject to the applicable upper-age relaxation.

Local Language

Candidates applying for a State/UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified opted local language.

Educational Qualification

Graduation in Any Discipline

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government is required.

Examination Pattern

The SBI Junior Associate recruitment process consists of Online Preliminary Examination, Online Main Examination and the specified Local Language Proficiency Test.

Phase - I

Online Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is an objective test used to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination.

Test Questions Marks Time Medium

  • There is negative marking of one-fourth of the mark assigned to a question for each wrong answer.

  • Adequate candidates, approximately 10 times the number of vacancies subject to availability, are shortlisted for the Main Examination based on aggregate marks.

Negative Marking

  • Negative marking applies to the objective tests in both Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth of the mark assigned to that question is deducted.
  • The penalty is applied while calculating the corrected score.
  • No penalty is imposed where a question is left unanswered.

Local Language Proficiency Test

The specified opted local language requirement is part of the SBI Junior Associate selection process.

Who Must Take It?

Provisionally selected candidates who have not studied the specified local language in Class 10th or 12th must appear for the test.

Language Certificate

Candidates producing a 10th or 12th standard marksheet/certificate showing that they studied the specified opted local language are not subjected to the language test.

Qualifying Nature

Candidates must score the minimum qualifying marks decided by SBI in the individual test, with the prescribed relaxation for eligible categories.

Failure

Candidates who fail the specified local language test or are not found proficient in the required language will be disqualified and will not be offered appointment.

Recruitment Process

Phase I — Prelims

Three objective tests with 100 marks and one hour duration.

Phase II — Mains

Four objective tests containing 190 questions for 200 marks.

Phase III — LLPT

Specified local language proficiency test for candidates who are not exempted through language-study proof.

Final Merit

Merit is drawn State-wise and category-wise on the basis of Main Examination aggregate marks.

Posting

Selected candidates are posted in the State/UT for which they applied, subject to the recruitment conditions.

Final Merit & Provisional Selection

  • Marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination are not added for selection.
  • Only aggregate marks obtained in the Main Examination are considered for preparation of the final merit list.
  • Merit lists are drawn State-wise and category-wise.
  • Candidates qualified in the Main Examination are placed according to their aggregate marks in descending order in their respective States and Categories.
  • Final selection is subject to verification of eligibility and information furnished in the online application.
  • Where applicable, final selection is also subject to qualifying the specified opted local language test.
  • A wait list of up to 50% of vacancies, State-category wise, may be maintained and is valid for one year from declaration of final result.

Special Provision for Trained SBI Apprentices

Bonus marks are prescribed for eligible trained apprentices of SBI.

Bonus Marks

Eligible trained SBI apprentices receive bonus marks equal to 2.5% of the maximum Main Examination marks, i.e. 5 marks out of 200.

Eligibility Condition

The apprentice's engagement start date must be on or after 01.01.2022 and the prescribed one-year branch experience must be completed in SBI on or before 31.08.2026, subject to the notification's conditions.

Training Completion

Successful completion includes passing the skill evaluation test and obtaining the joint National Apprenticeship Certificate as specified.

Important Application Instructions

  • Only one application should be submitted by a candidate.
  • If multiple applications are submitted, only the last valid completed application is retained and fees paid for other registrations are forfeited.
  • Once the application is filled and submitted, changes/editing are not permitted as prescribed by SBI.
  • Candidates should keep their registered email ID and mobile number active until declaration of results.
  • The photograph, signature, thumb impression and handwritten declaration must be clear.
  • After successful payment, an e-receipt and application form with fee details are generated and should be retained.
  • The printout of the application form is not to be sent to SBI.
  • Candidates may be given an option to provide Aadhaar details in the online application, and SBI may conduct online Aadhaar verification at different stages.

Examination Centre & Call Letter Instructions

  • Preliminary Examination is tentatively scheduled for September 2026 and Main Examination for November 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SBI Clerk / Junior Associate?

SBI Clerk refers to the Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) post in the clerical cadre of State Bank of India.

What is the educational qualification for SBI Clerk 2026?

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government is required.

What is the age limit for SBI Clerk 2026?

The candidate must be between 20 and 28 years as on 01.04.2026, subject to applicable relaxation.

What is the SBI Clerk application fee?

SC/ST/PwBD/XS/DXS candidates have Nil fee. General/OBC/EWS candidates have to pay ₹750. The fee is non-refundable.

How many questions are in SBI Clerk Prelims?

The Preliminary Examination has 100 questions for 100 marks and is conducted for one hour.

What is the SBI Clerk Prelims pattern?

English Language: 30 questions/30 marks/20 minutes; Numerical Ability: 35 questions/35 marks/20 minutes; Reasoning Ability: 35 questions/ 35 marks/20 minutes.

What is the SBI Clerk Mains pattern?

The Main Examination has 190 questions for 200 marks with a total duration of 2 hours 40 minutes.

What subjects are in SBI Clerk Mains?

General/Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude.

Is there negative marking in SBI Clerk?

Yes. One-fourth of the marks assigned to the question is deducted for each wrong answer in the objective tests.

Are Prelims marks counted in final selection?

No. Preliminary Examination marks are not added for selection. Only aggregate Main Examination marks are considered for the final merit list.

Is there a Local Language Test?

Yes, where applicable. Candidates who have not studied the specified local language in Class 10th or 12th must appear for the Local Language Proficiency Test after provisional selection and before joining.

Can the Local Language Test be avoided?

Candidates who produce a 10th or 12th standard marksheet/certificate showing that they studied the specified opted local language are not subjected to the language test.

What happens if I fail the Local Language Test?

Candidates who fail to qualify the specified local language test or are not found proficient in the required language are disqualified and are not offered appointment.

What is the starting basic pay of SBI Clerk?

The starting Basic Pay is ₹26,730, which includes two advance increments admissible to graduates.

What is the approximate starting salary?

The notification states that starting emoluments at a Metro such as Mumbai will be around ₹46,000 per month, inclusive of DA and other allowances at the current rate. Actual allowances vary by posting.

What is the probation period?

Newly appointed Junior Associates will be on probation for a minimum period of 6 months.

Can I apply for more than one State?

No. Candidates can apply for vacancies in one State/UT only under this recruitment project.

Can I change my State after applying?

The notification states that candidature is considered only against the State/UT for which the candidate has applied/opted and does not provide for changing the application details after submission.

Is there Inter-State Transfer?

There is no provision for Inter Circle Transfer / Inter State Transfer before completion of 5 years of service, except the exceptional cases specified by SBI under its transfer policy.
