The remaining objective tests are conducted in the examination mediums applicable to the State/UT selected by the candidate, as specified by SBI.

Preliminary Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Preliminary Examination consists of three objective tests: English Language, Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability.
  • Each test has a separate timing of 20 minutes.
  • The total Preliminary Examination duration is 1 hour.
  • There are no minimum qualifying marks prescribed for an individual test or for the aggregate score.
  • Section-wise marks will not be maintained.
  • There is negative marking for wrong answers in the objective tests. One-fourth of the mark assigned to the question is deducted for each wrong answer.
  • Adequate candidates in each category, approximately 10 times the number of vacancies subject to availability, are shortlisted for the Main Examination based on aggregate marks.

Main Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Main Examination consists of four objective tests.
  • Each test has a separate timing.
  • The Main Examination contains 190 questions for a maximum of 200 marks and has a total duration of 2 hours 40 minutes.
  • The four tests are General / Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude.
  • Candidates are required to score the minimum aggregate percentage marks prescribed by SBI.
  • SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/XS/DXS candidates receive the relaxation prescribed by SBI on the minimum aggregate qualifying marks.
  • No minimum qualifying marks are prescribed for individual subjects.
  • Section-wise marks will not be maintained.

Negative Marking

  • There is a penalty for wrong answers in the objective tests.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth of the mark assigned to that question is deducted.
  • The same negative-marking rule applies to the objective tests in both Preliminary and Main Examination.

Selection Process

The official notification describes the SBI Junior Associate selection process through Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and the specified Local Language Proficiency Test.

Stage 1 — Preliminary

Candidates appear in the Online Preliminary Examination. Candidates are shortlisted for the Main Examination based on the Preliminary aggregate score.

Stage 2 — Main

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Online Main Examination consisting of four separately timed objective tests.

Stage 3 — Local Language

Provisionally selected candidates who are not exempted through Class 10th/12th language-study proof must qualify the specified opted local language test before joining.

Final Merit

Preliminary marks are not added to final selection. Only aggregate Main Examination marks are considered for preparation of the final merit list.

State-wise Merit

Merit lists are drawn State-wise and category-wise. Selected candidates are posted in the State/UT for which they applied.

Final Selection — Important Points

  • Marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination are not added for selection.
  • Only aggregate marks obtained in the Main Examination are considered for preparation of the final merit list.
  • Merit list is drawn State-wise and category-wise.
  • Final selection is subject to verification of eligibility and information furnished in the online application.
  • Where applicable, final selection is subject to qualifying the specified opted local language test.
  • Candidates who fail the applicable local language test will not be offered appointment.

Local Language Requirement

The specified opted local language is an important part of the SBI Junior Associate selection process.

Language Proficiency

Candidates applying for vacancies of a particular State/UT should be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified opted local language.

Class 10th / 12th Proof

Candidates producing a 10th or 12th standard marksheet/certificate showing that they studied the specified opted local language are not required to undergo the language test.

When is the Test?

The Local Language Proficiency Test is conducted after qualifying the Online Main Examination but before joining the Bank.

Failure

Candidates who fail to qualify the specified local language test will not be offered appointment.

How many questions are in SBI Clerk Prelims?

What is the official SBI Clerk Main syllabus?

The Main Examination consists of General / Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude.
