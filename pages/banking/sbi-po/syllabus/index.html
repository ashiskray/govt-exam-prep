Report / Precis Writing

Preliminary Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Preliminary Examination consists of three objective tests: English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning Ability.
  • Each test has separate timing of 20 minutes.
  • The total Preliminary Examination duration is 1 hour.
  • The Preliminary Examination is conducted online for a maximum of 100 marks.
  • There will be no sectional cut-off in the Preliminary Examination.
  • Category-wise merit is drawn on the basis of aggregate marks scored in the Preliminary Examination.
  • Candidates numbering approximately 10 times the number of vacancies in each category are shortlisted for the Main Examination from the top of the merit list.

Main Examination — Official Conditions

  • The Main Examination consists of Objective Tests for 200 marks and a Descriptive Test for 30 marks.
  • The Objective Test consists of four sections with separate timing for every section.
  • The Objective Test contains 170 questions for 200 marks and has a total duration of 3 hours.
  • The four objective tests are Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation, General Awareness / Economy / Banking Knowledge, and English Language.
  • The Descriptive Test carries 30 marks and has a duration of 30 minutes.
  • The Descriptive Test is administered immediately after the Objective Test.
  • Candidates have to type the Descriptive Test answers on the computer.
  • Each Main Examination test/section and the Descriptive Paper has a minimum qualifying requirement as decided by SBI.
  • Candidates must also score the minimum aggregate qualifying score in the Main Examination as decided by SBI.
  • Up to approximately three times the category-wise vacancies are shortlisted for Phase III from the top of the category-wise merit list, subject to the prescribed qualifying conditions.

Negative Marking

  • There is a penalty for wrong answers in the Objective Tests of both Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • If a question is left blank, no penalty is imposed for that question.
  • The corrected score is calculated after applying the penalty for wrong answers.

Selection Process

The official notification describes the SBI PO recruitment through three phases: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Personal Interview.

Phase I — Preliminary

Candidates appear in the Online Preliminary Examination. Category-wise merit is prepared on the basis of aggregate Preliminary marks and approximately 10 times the vacancies are shortlisted for Main.

Phase II — Main

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Online Main Examination consisting of four objective tests followed immediately by the Descriptive Test.

Phase III — Psychometric

SBI conducts a Psychometric Test for personality profiling of candidates shortlisted for Phase III. Findings are placed before the Interview panel.

Group Exercise

Group Exercise carries a maximum of 20 marks. Minimum qualifying marks are decided by SBI.

Personal Interview

Personal Interview carries a maximum of 30 marks. Minimum qualifying marks are decided by SBI.

Final Merit

Phase-II and Phase-III marks are normalized to 100 for final merit. Preliminary marks are not added to the final merit list.

Final Selection — Important Points

  • Candidates have to qualify both Phase-II and Phase-III separately.
  • Marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination are not added for preparing the final merit list.
  • Marks obtained in the Main Examination, including both Objective and Descriptive Tests, are added with Phase-III marks for final merit.
  • Phase-II Main Examination marks and Phase-III marks are normalized to 100 marks.
  • Main Examination contributes 75 normalized marks.
  • Group Exercise & Interview contribute 25 normalized marks.
  • The final merit list is prepared after aggregating the converted Phase-II and Phase-III marks.
  • Provisional selection is made from the top merit-ranked candidates in each category.

Phase III — Marks Structure

The Phase III assessment consists of Psychometric Test, Group Exercise and Personal Interview. The notification specifies marks for Group Exercise and Interview.

Psychometric Test

The Bank conducts the Psychometric Test for personality profiling. All questions in the Psychometric Test are mandatory.

Group Exercise

Maximum Marks: 20. Minimum qualifying marks are as decided by the Bank.

Personal Interview

Maximum Marks: 30. Minimum qualifying marks are as decided by the Bank.

Phase III Total

Group Exercise + Interview = 50 marks.

Final Merit Normalization

The marks obtained in Phase-II and Phase-III are normalized to 100 marks for preparing the final merit list.

Application & Basic Recruitment Details

Application Period

Online registration and payment of fees are scheduled from 18.06.2026 to 08.07.2026.

Total Vacancies

The notification announces 1,500 vacancies, comprising 1,446 regular vacancies and 54 backlog vacancies.

Qualification

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government.

Age Limit

21 to 30 years as on 01.04.2026, subject to applicable upper-age relaxation.

Eligibility — Official Conditions

  • Candidate must be an eligible Indian citizen as specified in the notification.
  • Age must be not below 21 years and not above 30 years as on 01.04.2026.
  • Candidates must have been born not later than 01.04.2005 and not earlier than 02.04.1996, both days inclusive.
  • Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government is required as on 30.09.2026.
  • Final Year / Semester graduation candidates may apply provisionally, subject to producing proof of passing graduation on or before 30.09.2026 if called for interview.
  • Integrated Dual Degree candidates should ensure that the date of passing the IDD is on or before 30.09.2026.
  • Candidates possessing qualifications such as Medical, Engineering, Chartered Accountant and Cost Accountant are also eligible.
  • The date of passing the eligibility examination is the date appearing on the marksheet/provisional certificate issued by the University/Institute, subject to the notification's conditions.

Age Relaxation

OBC — Non-Creamy Layer

Upper-age relaxation: 3 years.

SC / ST

Upper-age relaxation: 5 years.

PwBD

PwBD (UR/EWS): 10 years; PwBD (OBC): 13 years; PwBD (SC/ST): 15 years.

Ex-Servicemen / Eligible Officers

Upper-age relaxation: 5 years, subject to the conditions specified in the notification.

Upload Requirements

The notification requires candidates to scan and upload their photograph, signature, left-hand thumb impression and hand-written declaration, and also capture a live photograph during registration.

Photograph

Recent passport-style colour photograph. Preferred dimensions 200 × 230 pixels; file size 20–50 KB.

Signature

Black ink on white paper. Preferred dimensions 140 × 60 pixels; file size 10–20 KB. CAPITAL LETTER signature is not accepted.

Left-Hand Thumb Impression

JPG/JPEG; preferred 240 × 240 pixels at 200 DPI; file size 20–50 KB. Right thumb may be used if the candidate does not have a left-hand thumb.

Hand-Written Declaration

English, clearly written with black ink. JPG/JPEG; preferred 800 × 400 pixels at 200 DPI; file size 50–100 KB. CAPITAL LETTERS are not accepted.

Live Photograph

A live photograph must also be captured and uploaded through a webcam or mobile phone during registration.

Hand-Written Declaration

The declaration specified in the notification is:

“I________(Name of the candidate), _______(Date of Birth) hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required. The signature, photograph and left-hand thumb impression is of mine”.

Identity & Examination Security

Photo Identity Proof

Candidates must bring an original photo identity proof and a self-attested photocopy as prescribed in the notification.

Biometric Verification

SBI may capture photograph, thumb impression and/or IRIS Scan at various stages for biometric verification.

Electronic Devices

Mobile phones, pagers, smart watches, cameras, Bluetooth devices, storage media and related electronic devices are not allowed inside the examination, interview or group exercise premises.

Misconduct

Unfair means, impersonation, misbehaviour or other improper means may result in disqualification, debarment and other action.

Joining, Training & Career Path

Important service-related provisions stated in the official notification.

Basic Banking Course

Selected candidates are provided basic banking knowledge through an online course which they must complete before joining.

Service Bond

At joining, selected candidates execute a bond of ₹2.00 lakh to serve the Bank for a minimum period of three years.

Probation

Selected candidates are designated as Probationary Officers and may remain on probation for two years, subject to continuous assessment.

Career Growth

Candidates qualifying the prescribed assessment standards may be confirmed in Junior Management Grade Scale-I. SBI provides opportunities for career growth, including overseas postings.

Salary & Pay Scale

Post Scale of Pay Starting Basic Pay
Probationary Officer ₹48,480-₹85,920 ₹48,480 + 4 Advance Increments

The starting basic pay is ₹48,480 plus four advance increments. The officer is eligible for DA, HRA, CCA, PF, Contributory Pension Fund under NPS, LFC, Medical Facility, Lease Rental Facility and other allowances and perquisites as per rules. The approximate CTC at the initial scale of pay at Mumbai Centre is ₹21.97 Lacs.

CIBIL Requirement

  • Candidates who have defaulted in repayment under lending arrangements with Banks, NBFCs or Financial Institutions, including credit-card dues, must regularize or repay outstanding dues on or before joining.
  • If the adverse CIBIL status has not been updated by the date of joining, the candidate must get the status updated or produce NOC/documentary evidence from the concerned lender.
  • Failure to regularize/repay dues and submit satisfactory proof can render the candidate ineligible for appointment and the offer may be withdrawn/cancelled.
  • Candidates are advised to check and confirm their CIBIL score/status before applying.
