Post Details

SBI recruits candidates for the Probationary Officer position in Junior Management Grade Scale-I.

Probationary Officer

Selected candidates join State Bank of India as Probationary Officers and are designated in Junior Management Grade Scale-I.

Posting

Selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India. SBI may allot or transfer officers to Circles/offices in India according to service requirements.

Career Growth

The notification highlights opportunities for accelerated career progression, leadership development, international exposure and advancement to higher management grades.

Transfer Policy

SBI reserves the right of allotment/transfer to any Circle or office in India and may depute officers to associates, subsidiaries or other organisations according to service requirements.

The starting basic pay is ₹48,480, plus four advance increments. The notification lists benefits and facilities including DA, HRA, CCA, Provident Fund, Contributory Pension Fund under NPS, Leave Fare Concession, Medical Facility and Lease Rental Facility, along with other allowances and perquisites according to Bank rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements prescribed in the official notification.

Qualification

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government.

Nationality

The notification invites online applications from eligible Indian citizens for appointment as Probationary Officers.

Age

Minimum 21 years and maximum 30 years as on 01.04.2026, subject to the applicable upper-age relaxation.

Final Year Candidates

Final year/semester graduation candidates may apply provisionally, subject to producing proof of passing graduation by 30.09.2026 if called for interview.

Educational Qualification

Graduation in Any Discipline

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government is required.

Final Year / Semester Candidates

Candidates who are in the Final Year/Semester of their Graduation may also apply provisionally. If called for interview, they must produce proof of having passed the graduation examination on or before 30.09.2026.

Integrated Dual Degree

Candidates having an Integrated Dual Degree (IDD) certificate should ensure that the date of passing the IDD is on or before 30.09.2026.

Professional Qualifications

Candidates possessing qualifications such as Medical, Engineering, Chartered Accountant, Cost Accountant and other eligible equivalent qualifications are also eligible, subject to the notification.

Date of Passing

The date of passing the eligibility examination will be the date appearing on the marksheet or provisional certificate issued by the University/Institute. Where the result is posted on the University/ Institute website, the prescribed certificate indicating the date of result publication is accepted.

Age Limit

21 – 30 Years

The candidate must not be below 21 years and not above 30 years as on 01.04.2026.

Candidates must have been born not later than 01.04.2005 and not earlier than 02.04.1996, both days inclusive.

Examination Pattern

The SBI PO recruitment process consists of three phases: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Personal Interview.

Phase - I

Online Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is an objective test used to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination.

Test Questions Marks Time
English Language 40 40 20 Minutes
Quantitative Aptitude 30 30 20 Minutes
Reasoning Ability 30 30 20 Minutes
Total 100 100 1 Hour
  • Each section has separate timing.
  • There is no sectional cut-off in the Preliminary Examination.
  • One-fourth of the marks assigned to the question is deducted for a wrong answer.
  • A question left blank carries no penalty.
  • Category-wise merit is prepared on the basis of aggregate Preliminary marks.
  • Candidates numbering approximately 10 times the vacancies in each category are shortlisted for the Main Examination.

Preliminary Examination Subjects

Subject-wise structure of the SBI PO Preliminary Examination.

Reasoning Ability

30 questions, 30 marks and 20 minutes.

Phase - II

Online Main Examination

The Main Examination contains an objective test for 200 marks followed immediately by a descriptive test for 30 marks.

Test Questions Marks Time
Reasoning & Computer Aptitude 40 60 50 Minutes
Data Analysis & Interpretation 30 60 45 Minutes
General Awareness / Economy / Banking Knowledge 60 60 45 Minutes
English Language 40 20 40 Minutes
Objective Total 170 200 3 Hours
Descriptive Test — 30 30 Minutes
Grand Total 230 230 3 Hours 30 Minutes
  • The Objective Test has separate timing for every section.
  • The Descriptive Test is administered immediately after the Objective Test.
  • Descriptive answers are typed on the computer.
  • Each Main Examination test/section and the Descriptive Paper have minimum qualifying requirements as decided by SBI.
  • Candidates must also score the minimum aggregate qualifying score decided by SBI.
  • Approximately up to three times the category-wise vacancies may be shortlisted for Phase III.

Main Examination Subjects

Major sections included in the Online Main Examination.

Reasoning & Computer Aptitude

40 questions, 60 marks and 50 minutes.

Data Analysis & Interpretation

30 questions, 60 marks and 45 minutes.

General Awareness / Economy / Banking Knowledge

60 questions, 60 marks and 45 minutes.

English Language

40 questions, 20 marks and 40 minutes.

Descriptive Test — Communication Skills

The Descriptive Test is conducted immediately after the Objective Main Examination and candidates type their answers on the computer.

Emails

One question is to be attempted out of two options.

Situation Analysis

One question is to be attempted out of two options.

Report / Precis Writing

Either report writing or precis writing, with one option to be attempted out of two.

Descriptive Paper

30 Marks • 30 Minutes

Negative Marking

  • Negative marking applies to the Objective Tests in both Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • For every wrong answer, one-fourth of the marks assigned to that question is deducted.
  • If a question is left blank, no penalty is imposed.

Phase III — Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Interview

Candidates shortlisted from the Main Examination proceed to the third phase of the selection process.

Psychometric Test

The Bank conducts the Psychometric Test for personality profiling. Its findings are placed before the Interview panel.

Group Exercise

Maximum marks: 20. Minimum qualifying marks are decided by SBI.

Personal Interview

Maximum marks: 30. Minimum qualifying marks are decided by SBI.

Phase III Total

Group Exercise + Interview = 50 marks.

Final Merit & Selection

Component Maximum Marks Normalized Marks
Main Examination — Objective + Descriptive 230 75
Group Exercise + Interview 50 25
Total 280 100
  • Candidates must qualify Phase II and Phase III separately.
  • Preliminary Examination marks are not added to the final merit list.
  • Main Examination marks and Phase III marks are normalized to 100 marks.
  • Main Examination contributes 75 normalized marks.
  • Group Exercise & Interview contribute 25 normalized marks.
  • The final merit list is prepared by aggregating the converted Phase II and Phase III marks.
  • Provisional selection is made from the top merit-ranked candidates in each category.

Joining, Training & Career Path

Important service-related information stated in the official notification.

Basic Banking Course

Selected candidates are provided basic banking knowledge through an online course, which must be completed before joining.

Service Bond

At joining, selected candidates execute a bond of ₹2,00,000 to serve the Bank for a minimum period of three years.

Probation

Newly joined Probationary Officers may be on probation for two years and are subjected to continuous assessment.

Career Growth

Candidates who meet the Bank's assessment standards are confirmed in Junior Management Grade Scale-I. SBI provides opportunities for career growth and overseas postings.

Examination Centres

Online examinations are conducted at venues across many centres in India.

  • The notification provides a tentative list of centres for Preliminary and Main Examination.
  • No request for change of Examination Centre, Date or Session is entertained.
  • SBI reserves the right to cancel centres, add centres or allot a centre different from the candidate's choice.
  • Main Examination centres will be limited.
  • Candidates appear at the examination centre at their own risk and expense.
  • Unruly behaviour or misconduct at the examination centre may result in cancellation of candidature or disqualification.

PwBD / Scribe Provisions

The official notification provides reservation, scribe and compensatory-time provisions subject to prescribed conditions.

PwBD Reservation

4% horizontal reservation is provided to Persons with Benchmark Disabilities as prescribed under the RPwD Act, 2016.

Scribe Facility

Eligible candidates with the prescribed disability may use a scribe subject to the notification's conditions and undertakings.

Compensatory Time

Eligible candidates using the scribe facility are entitled to 20 minutes compensatory time for every hour of the examination.

Disability Certificate

Candidates claiming PwBD benefits must submit a valid certificate issued by the competent authority as prescribed.

Maximum Number of Chances

The notification specifies the maximum permissible attempts for SBI PO.

Category Maximum Permissible Chances
UR / EWS 6
UR (PwBD) / EWS (PwBD) / OBC / OBC (PwBD) 9
SC / SC (PwBD) / ST / ST (PwBD) No Restriction
  • Candidates who have already appeared for the maximum permissible number of chances are not eligible to apply.
  • Appearing in the Main Examination counts as a chance.
  • Appearing in the Preliminary Examination does not count as a chance.

CIBIL / Credit History Requirement

Candidates who have defaulted in repayment under lending arrangements with Banks, NBFCs or Financial Institutions, including credit-card dues, must regularize or repay outstanding dues on or before joining. If the adverse CIBIL status has not been updated, the candidate may need to produce the required NOC/documentary evidence from the lender. Failure to satisfy the requirement may render the candidate ineligible for appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SBI PO?

SBI PO is the Probationary Officer recruitment conducted by State Bank of India for appointment as Probationary Officers.

How many SBI PO vacancies are there in 2026?

The notification provides 1,446 regular vacancies and 54 backlog vacancies, making a total of 1,500 vacancies.

What is the educational qualification for SBI PO 2026?

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government is required.

Can final-year graduation students apply for SBI PO?

Yes. Final-year/semester candidates may apply provisionally, provided they produce proof of passing graduation by 30.09.2026 if called for interview.

What is the SBI PO age limit?

The candidate must be between 21 and 30 years as on 01.04.2026, subject to applicable relaxation.

What is the SBI PO application fee?

The application fee is ₹750 for General/EWS/OBC candidates and Nil for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. The fee is non-refundable.

How many questions are in SBI PO Prelims?

The Preliminary Examination has 100 questions for 100 marks and is conducted for one hour.

What is the SBI PO Prelims pattern?

English Language: 40 questions/40 marks/20 minutes; Quantitative Aptitude: 30 questions/30 marks/20 minutes; Reasoning Ability: 30 questions/30 marks/20 minutes.

What is the SBI PO Mains pattern?

The Main Objective Examination has 170 questions for 200 marks and lasts three hours, followed immediately by a 30-mark descriptive test of 30 minutes.

What subjects are in SBI PO Mains?

Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation, General Awareness/Economy/Banking Knowledge, and English Language. There is also a Descriptive Test on communication skills.

What is the SBI PO Descriptive Test?

It is a 30-mark, 30-minute test covering emails, situation analysis and either report writing or precis writing, with the specified choices in the notification.

Is there negative marking in SBI PO?

Yes. One-fourth of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in the Objective Tests. There is no penalty for a question left blank.

Are SBI PO Prelims marks counted in final selection?

No. Preliminary Examination marks are not added to the final merit list.

What is Phase III of SBI PO?

Phase III consists of a Psychometric Test, Group Exercise and Personal Interview.

How are SBI PO final marks calculated?

Main Examination marks and Phase III marks are normalized to 100. The Main Examination contributes 75 normalized marks and Group Exercise plus Interview contribute 25 normalized marks.

What is the SBI PO starting basic pay?

The starting basic pay is ₹48,480 plus four advance increments. The approximate initial CTC at Mumbai is ₹21.97 lakh as stated in the notification.

Is there a bond in SBI PO?

Yes. Selected candidates execute a bond of ₹2 lakh to serve the Bank for a minimum period of three years.

What is the SBI PO probation period?

Selected candidates may be on probation for two years and are subject to continuous assessment during the probation period.

Can SBI PO officers be posted anywhere in India?

Yes. The notification states that selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India and SBI reserves the right of allotment and transfer according to service requirements.

How many attempts are allowed for SBI PO?

UR/EWS candidates have 6 chances; UR(PwBD)/EWS(PwBD)/OBC/OBC(PwBD) have 9 chances; SC/SC(PwBD)/ST/ST(PwBD) have no restriction. Appearing in Mains counts as a chance, while appearing in Prelims does not.

Where can I check official SBI PO updates?

All further announcements, updates and corrigenda are published on SBI's official career portal.
