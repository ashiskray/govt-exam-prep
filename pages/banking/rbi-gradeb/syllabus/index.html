RBI Grade B Syllabus

Explore the official syllabus and examination structure for RBI Officers in Grade ‘B’ (DR) — General Cadre, Panel Year 2025. The syllabus and examination details on this page are based on the official Reserve Bank of India Services Board notification and Appendix-II.

Economic and Social Issues — Phase-II

Official Syllabus

Growth and Development
Indian Economy
Globalization
Social Structure in India

Growth and Development: Measurement of growth; National Income and per capita income; Poverty Alleviation and Employment Generation in India; Sustainable Development and Environmental issues.

Indian Economy: Economic History of India; changes in Industrial and Labour Policy; Monetary and Fiscal Policy since reforms of 1991; priorities and recommendations of Economic Survey and Union Budget; Indian Money and Financial Markets and their linkages with the economy; role of Indian banks and Reserve Bank in the development process; Public Finance; Political Economy; Industrial Developments in India; Indian Agriculture; Services sector in India.

Globalization: Opening up of the Indian Economy; Balance of Payments; Export-Import Policy; International Economic Institutions including IMF and World Bank; WTO; Regional Economic Co-operation; International Economic Issues.

Social Structure in India: Multiculturalism; Demographic Trends; Urbanisation and Migration; Gender Issues; Social Justice.

English — Writing Skills

Official Syllabus Scope

Writing Skills
Expression
Understanding of the Topic

The official notification states that the English paper is framed to assess writing skills including expression and understanding of the topic. The paper is descriptive and is to be typed with the help of the keyboard. It carries 100 marks and has a duration of 90 minutes.

General Finance — Phase-II

Official Syllabus

Financial System
Financial Markets
Financial Risk Management
Derivatives
International Banking
Financial Inclusion
Union Budget
Accounting and Financial Statements
Inflation

Financial System: Structure and Functions of Financial Institutions; Functions of Reserve Bank of India; Banking System in India — Structure and Developments; Financial Institutions such as SIDBI, EXIM Bank, NABARD, NHB and NaBFID; recent developments in the global financial system and their impact on the Indian financial system; role of Information Technology in Banking and Finance; Non-Banking System; Developments in Digital Payments.

Financial Markets: Primary and Secondary Markets including Forex, Money, Bond and Equity markets; functions, instruments and recent developments.

General Topics: Financial Risk Management; Basics of Derivatives; Global financial markets and International Banking — broad trends and latest developments; Financial Inclusion; alternate sources of finance; private and social cost-benefit; Public-Private Partnership; Corporate Governance in Banking Sector; Union Budget — concepts, approach and broad trends; Basics of Accounting and Financial Statements including Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Cash Flow Statements and Ratio Analysis; Inflation — definition, trends, estimates, consequences and remedies, WPI-CPI components and trends, and balancing inflation and growth through monetary and fiscal policies.

Management — Phase-II

Official Syllabus

Management Fundamentals
Organizational Behaviour
Motivation
Leadership
Emotional Intelligence
Interpersonal Relationships
Conflict Management
Organizational Change
Organizational Development
Ethics
Corporate Governance

Fundamentals of Management & Organizational Behaviour: Introduction to management; evolution of management thought: Scientific, Administrative, Human Relations and Systems approach; management functions; managerial roles; Nudge theory; meaning and concept of organizational behaviour.

Personality and Perception: Personality; factors affecting personality; Big Five model; reinforcement; perception and perceptual errors.

Motivation: Concept and importance; Maslow's Need Theory; Alderfer's ERG Theory; McClelland's Theory of Needs; Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory; Adams' Equity Theory; Vroom's Expectancy Theory.

Leadership and Emotional Intelligence: Trait, Behavioural, Contingency, Charismatic, Transactional and Transformational leadership theories; Emotional Intelligence — concept, importance and dimensions.

Interpersonal Relationships and Conflict: Transactional Analysis; Johari Window; conflict — concept, sources, types and management.

Organizational Change and Development: Organizational change; Kurt Lewin Theory of Change; Organizational Development; strategies for change; planned change theories including Lewin's Change Model, Action Research Model and Positive Model.

Ethics and Corporate Governance: Meaning of ethics; why ethical problems occur in business; Utilitarianism; Rights and Duties; Justice and Fairness; Ethics of Care; Virtue Ethics; Teleological theories; Egoism; Relativism; moral issues in business; ethical principles; organization structure and ethics; role of Board of Directors; ethics programmes; Code of Ethics; Code of Conduct; Corporate Governance factors and mechanisms.

Communication: Steps in communication process; communication channels; oral versus written communication; verbal versus non-verbal communication; upward, downward and lateral communication; barriers to communication; role of Information Technology.

Exam Pattern Highlights

Two-Phase Written Examination

Selection for Grade B General is conducted through Phase-I, Phase-II and Interview.

Phase-I — 200 Marks

One objective paper containing General Awareness, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning, with 120 minutes total time.

Phase-II — 300 Marks

Economic and Social Issues, English Writing Skills, and General Finance and Management together carry 300 marks.

Interview — 75 Marks

Candidates shortlisted from Phase-II are called for an Interview carrying 75 marks.

Official Syllabus Scope — Important Note

  • The official notification identifies General Awareness, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning as the four Phase-I tests.
  • Phase-I is one objective paper carrying 200 marks with a total time of 120 minutes, with separate time allotted to each test.
  • The detailed chapter-wise syllabus published in Appendix-II is for Phase-II, covering Economic and Social Issues, English Writing Skills, and General Finance and Management.
  • Paper-I and Paper-III of Phase-II each contain 50% objective and 50% descriptive components.
  • Paper-II is English Writing Skills and is descriptive, with answers typed using the keyboard.
  • The official notification does not specify the individual Phase-I marks or number of questions for each of the four tests in Appendix-II.
  • Therefore, this page does not invent a chapter-wise Phase-I syllabus or test-wise marks that are not stated in the official notification.

Medium of Examination

English Paper

The English test/paper is conducted in English. The Phase-II English Writing Skills paper is specifically an English writing assessment.

Other Papers

The official notification states that all question papers in both phases, except the test of English, are set bilingually in Hindi and English.

Typing in Phase-II

Phase-II Paper-I and Paper-III descriptive answers are typed with the help of the keyboard. Hindi typing may use Inscript or Remington (GAIL) keyboard layouts.

Phase-I Examination — Official Conditions

  • Phase-I is a single objective paper carrying 200 marks.
  • The paper consists of General Awareness, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning.
  • A total time of 120 minutes is provided, with separate time allotted for each test.
  • Candidates have to secure minimum marks separately for each test as well as in aggregate, as prescribed by the Board.
  • Candidates meeting the prescribed test-wise requirements are shortlisted for Phase-II based on aggregate Phase-I marks and the cut-off decided by the Board.
  • If Phase-I is conducted in multiple sessions, corrected scores are normalized using the equipercentile method.

Phase-II Examination — Official Conditions

  • Phase-II consists of three papers carrying a combined 300 marks.
  • Paper-I is Economic and Social Issues and contains 50% objective and 50% descriptive components.
  • Paper-II is English (Writing Skills), descriptive, carrying 100 marks and having 90 minutes duration.
  • Paper-III is General Finance and Management and contains 50% objective and 50% descriptive components.
  • For Paper-I and Paper-III, six descriptive questions are asked and four must be attempted.
  • The first four descriptive answers are evaluated if a candidate answers more than four.
  • Candidates are shortlisted for Interview based on aggregate Phase-II marks. The Interview carries 75 marks.

RBI Grade B Selection Process

The official notification provides a three-stage selection process for the Grade B General stream.

Stage 1 — Phase-I

Candidates appear in the single 200-mark objective Phase-I paper. Candidates satisfying the prescribed test-wise and aggregate requirements are shortlisted for Phase-II.

Stage 2 — Phase-II

Shortlisted candidates appear in Economic and Social Issues, English Writing Skills, and General Finance and Management.

Stage 3 — Interview

Candidates sufficiently high in Phase-II aggregate marks are shortlisted for the Interview, which carries 75 marks.

Final Merit

Final selection is based on Phase-II examination marks and Interview marks, along with grace marks as applicable under the rules.

Important Syllabus Notes

  • This syllabus page specifically presents the Grade ‘B’ (DR) General stream under Appendix-II.

  • The Phase-I notification specifies the four tests and the total time, but does not provide test-wise marks or a detailed chapter list in Appendix-II.
